East Bengaluru infrastructure projects: where does each one stand in October 2026?

a&k
acre&key
Acrenkey Realty LLP • 19 Oct 2026 • 13 min read
Share:

Short answer: Of the big east Bengaluru infrastructure projects, only the Purple Line metro and the STRR's Dobaspet–Hoskote section are running. The Blue Line's Silk Board–KR Puram stretch is reportedly targeting May 2027. Suburban rail to Whitefield is still acquiring land. PRR-1's eastern packages aren't tendered, and the KR Puram–Hoskote metro is only a project report.

Key takeaways
  • Running today: Purple Line (Whitefield–Challaghatta), STRR Dobaspet–Hoskote, the Karnataka section of the Bengaluru–Chennai Expressway.
  • Next to open, by targets: Blue Line Silk Board–KR Puram (May 2027, as reported), then KR Puram–Hebbal (December 2027 to March 2028).
  • Slow: suburban Corridor-3 (Kengeri–Whitefield) is at land acquisition, with no revised deadline given to Parliament.
  • Roads: PRR-1 Packages 2 and 3 (Ballari Road–Old Madras Road–Channasandra) are due for tender by November, as reported. Package 3 has 28% of its land.
  • Our Stage–Distance score turns each project into a number you can use in a price conversation.

Worked example 1: EMIs paid while you wait for the Blue Line

Illustrative: an ₹80 lakh loan at 8.5% over 20 years has an EMI of ₹69,426. You buy in October 2026 near an outer ring road station.

If the stretch opens Months from Oct 2026 EMIs paid before opening
May 2027 (Silk Board–KR Puram, BMRCL target as reported) 7 7 × ₹69,426 = ₹4,85,982
March 2028 (KR Puram–Hebbal, upper target) 17 17 × ₹69,426 = ₹11,80,242

Those EMIs come with today's commute. If the metro is why you're paying more, weigh the extra price against the wait.

Worked example 2: scoring two flats with the Stage–Distance score

Our framework scores each project you're counting on. Stage: running 5, safety-cleared 4, under construction 3, approved 2, proposed 1. Distance from your gate: under 1 km is 3, 1–3 km is 2, over 3 km is 1. Multiply the two and add up the projects.

Flat A, near Marathahalli Flat B, on Old Madras Road past KR Puram
Purple Line Running (5) × 1–3 km (2) = 10 Running (5) × over 3 km (1) = 5
Blue Line ORR Under construction (3) × under 1 km (3) = 9 Under construction (3) × over 3 km (1) = 3
KR Puram–Hoskote metro n/a Proposed (1) × under 1 km (3) = 3
PRR-1 Package 3 n/a Approved, land 28% (2) × 1–3 km (2) = 4
Total 19 15

Flat B's brochure lists more projects, but A scores higher because its projects are further along and closer. Use the score to question a higher price for "connectivity", not to predict prices.

Checklist before paying for "upcoming connectivity"

  • [ ] Who gave the target date: the agency, a minister or a news report
  • [ ] The slip so far for that project
  • [ ] Walking distance to the nearest running station, measured at peak hour
  • [ ] Truck and noise exposure near STRR, the expressway and Old Madras Road

Frequently asked questions

BMRCL was reported in October 2026 to be targeting May 2027 for the Central Silk Board–KR Puram stretch, with trials from November 2026. The minister said "mid-2027". Neither is a safety-cleared opening date, so plan on the later end.

Not yet. The Purple Line ends at Whitefield (Kadugodi). A KR Puram–Hoskote line of about 16–17 km with 11 stations along Old Madras Road is at the project report stage, as reported in October 2026. It has no approval or funding yet.

There's no date. Corridor-3 (Kengeri–Whitefield, 35.52 km) has an approved alignment, and land acquisition is under way. The Railways minister's July 2026 Lok Sabha reply gave no revised completion timeline. The project's original target was October 2026.

Yes. The roughly 80 km Dobaspet–Hoskote section via Doddaballapura and Devanahalli has been open for over two years. A further 21 km stretch towards the Tamil Nadu border was 94.89% complete and targeted for November 2026, according to a reported Rajya Sabha reply.

PRR-1, now called the Bengaluru Business Corridor, is 73.4 km from Tumakuru Road to Hosur Road. Package 1 in the north has started. The eastern Packages 2 and 3 are due for tender by November 2026, with 70% and 28% of land in possession, as The Hindu reported.

Not yet. BMRCL's project report for the 37.8 km Sarjapura–Hebbal line is dated February 2026 and awaits central approval. Soil testing began in October 2026, and Deccan Herald reported the line may open only by 2033. Treat it as years away.

Pay for what's running and close by. Projects under construction deserve some weight; proposed ones deserve little. Score each project by stage and distance, and work out what you'll pay in EMIs before the date you're relying on.
