New RERA-registered supply in Bengaluru by taluk, 2020–2026: where are projects being added?

a&k
acre&key
Acrenkey Realty LLP • 19 Oct 2026 • 8 min read
Share:

Short answer: New RERA projects in Bengaluru are concentrated in three taluks. From 2020 to 15 Oct 2026, Karnataka RERA approved 2,692 residential, plotted and mixed projects across seven Bengaluru taluks. Bengaluru East (643), Anekal (603) and Bengaluru South (556) made up two-thirds. January–September 2026 approvals were 20% below the same months of 2025.

Key takeaways
  • 2,692 approvals in seven taluks from 2020 to 15 Oct 2026, commercial projects excluded.
  • East, Anekal and South lead, with about 24%, 22% and 21% of the total.
  • 2026 is slower: 309 approvals in Jan–Sep against 387 a year earlier. Hosakote was the exception, up from 30 to 40.
  • The outskirts are mostly plots: 78% of Devanahalli's approvals and 52% of Hosakote's are plotted developments, against 8% in Bengaluru East.
  • Projects, not homes. The register's list view doesn't give unit counts, so this counts projects.

Supply numbers from consultants count launched units across the city. This study uses the regulator's own register instead, taluk by taluk, so you can see where new registered projects are actually being added near you. The table behind it is published with this page.

How did we count?

  • Source: the Karnataka RERA "View All Projects" list (K-RERA) for seven taluks: Anekal, Bengaluru East, North and South, Yelahanka (Bengaluru Urban), and Devanahalli and Hosakote (Bengaluru Rural). Captured 15 Oct 2026.
  • Counted: projects with status APPROVED, by the register's "approved on" date, 1 Jan 2020 to 15 Oct 2026. Residential/group housing, plotted and mixed developments; commercial excluded. Rejected, withdrawn, revoked and transferred entries are left out.
  • Not counted: units, sizes or prices. The list view doesn't show them, and we don't name projects or promoters.
  • Duplicates: removed by acknowledgement number. The same base list gives 4,529 approved non-commercial projects in all years, matching our RERA completion-date study.

Did registrations slow in 2026?

Yes, in most taluks. Comparing the same nine months:

It comes as unsold stock in the city rose 35% in a year by ANAROCK's count, though consultants still counted Q3 launches above sales (see our Q3 2026 market report). Registration counts can also move with how fast the authority processes applications, which this data can't separate.

Worked example 1: monthly pace

Period Approvals Months Per month
2025 505 12 about 42
1 Jan–15 Oct 2026 333 9.5 about 35

At the 2026 pace, the seven taluks would finish the year at roughly 35 × 12 = 420 approvals, below every year since 2022 except 2024 (421). That's a projection, not a count.

Worked example 2: reading "lots of new supply" near you

Flat projects 2020–26 90 The relevant number if you're buying a flat

So for a flat buyer, Hosakote's competing supply is 90 projects over almost seven years, not 186. Our Hoskote guide covers the rest.

The Count–Kind–Clock check (our framework)

Before you treat "supply" as good or bad for a purchase:

  1. Count. How many registered projects in your taluk, and is the number rising or falling?
  2. Kind. How many are the same type as yours: flats, plots or villas?
  3. Clock. When are they due? Check each competing project's current completion date on RERA, and its record of extensions.
