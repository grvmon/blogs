Section 14(3) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 says that if any structural defect, or any other defect in workmanship, quality or provision of services, or any other obligation of the promoter under the agreement for sale, is brought to the promoter's notice by the allottee within five years of the date of handing over possession, the promoter has to rectify it without further charge within thirty days. If the promoter fails to do so, the allottee is entitled to appropriate compensation under the Act.

This notice is given within five years of the date of handing over possession. Please:

1. Acknowledge this notice in writing.
2. Inspect the defects on a date you confirm with me in advance.
3. Rectify all the defects listed above, without charge, within thirty days of receiving this notice.

If the defects are not rectified within that time, I reserve my right to approach the Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority and its adjudicating officer under the Act.

Yours faithfully,

[Signature]
[Your full name]

Send it in a way you can prove: email to the address in the agreement, and registered post or courier with proof of delivery. Keep copies and note the date the promoter received it. The thirty days run from then.
