LETTER 3: DRAFT COMPLAINT TO KARNATAKA RERA (FORM 'N', RULE 29)
Template from acre&key (acrenkey.com/blogs). Not legal advice. Have a lawyer review it before you file.
This draft follows the headings of Form 'N' in the Karnataka Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Rules, 2017, notified on 10 July 2017. Prepare your text in this layout, then enter it in the complaint form on rera.karnataka.gov.in or file it as the Authority directs. Check the portal for the current filing and fee process before you file.
If you are only claiming compensation under sections 12, 14, 18 or 19, the Rules provide a separate application to the adjudicating officer in Form 'O' (rule 30). Ask your lawyer which one fits your case.
