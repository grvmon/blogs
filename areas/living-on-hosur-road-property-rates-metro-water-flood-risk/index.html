Living on Hosur Road: property rates, metro, water and flood risk

a&k
acre&key
Acrenkey Realty LLP • 19 Oct 2026 • 12 min read
Share:

Short answer: Hosur Road property rates change sharply along its length. Guidance values fall from ₹1,98,900 per sq m on the inner road to ₹42,000 in Bommanahalli and ₹28,320 in Singasandra. At the Electronic City end, Knight Frank put flats at ₹5,700 to ₹8,600 per sq ft in H1 2026. The Yellow Line has run along the road since August 2025.

Key takeaways
  • No single figure: ANAROCK lists Hosur Road in its South zone without a rate. The nearest consultant numbers are for Electronic City.
  • Steep fall in floors: from ₹1,98,900 per sq m near Lakkasandra to ₹28,320 in the Singasandra corporation area.
  • Metro running: the Yellow Line, RV Road to Bommasandra, opened on 10 August 2025.
  • Two sets of rules: PIN 560068 is fully in GBA; PIN 560099, further out, is wholly outside it.
  • Our BAND check: boundary, access, noise, drains.

Hosur Road runs south-east from the city centre past Madiwala and Silk Board, through Bommanahalli, Hongasandra, Singasandra and Electronic City, towards Bommasandra, Chandapura and Attibele. It's an office and industrial corridor as much as a residential one. This guide uses official records and named sources, and says where data runs out.

Who governs Hosur Road?

It changes along the road. The inner and middle stretches are in the South City Corporation: PIN 560068 (Bommanahalli side) and PIN 560100 (Electronic City, with Singasandra and Chikkathoguru wards) are fully inside GBA. PIN 560099, around Bommasandra and Chandapura, is wholly outside, and PIN 562107 is only about 5% inside. Out there you're in Anekal taluk, where a town council or gram panchayat may issue the khata.

Inside GBA, stamp duty, cess, surcharge and registration add up to 7.6%; outside, 7.65%. Check your plot with our ward and corporation lookup and our guide to BDA, BMRDA, GBA or panchayat approvals.

Beyond Electronic City, look up Bommasandra, Chandapura and Attibele survey numbers on Kaveri, as rules and lines differ outside GBA. Kaveri can also add floor, amenity and parking amounts for flats.

Market prices

Knight Frank's H1 2026 report put Electronic City at ₹5,700 to ₹8,600 per sq ft, up 10% in a year, and ANAROCK's Q1 2026 base rate there was ₹7,200. Neither gives a figure for Hosur Road as a whole. Use these for the Electronic City end only. Our Electronic City guide has more.

Rents

ANAROCK put 2BHK rents in Electronic City at ₹20,000 to ₹31,000 a month in Q1 2026. We didn't find a figure for the inner road.

What's being built

The outer stretch is in Anekal taluk, where Karnataka RERA approved 603 projects between January 2020 and mid-October 2026 (acre&key analysis of the RERA register). That covers far more than this road, and the register has no locality field. Use our RERA project review guide before you book.

Is there a metro on Hosur Road?

Yes. The Yellow Line runs from RV Road (Ragigudda) to Bommasandra, over 19 km with 16 stations, and was inaugurated on 10 August 2025 (PIB). Most of it follows Hosur Road.

Line Stations along the road (station names per news reports) Status, 19 Oct 2026

The Blue Line date is from the Bengaluru development minister, reported in October 2026 (The Hindu, news). See our metro lines guide.

Where does Hosur Road get its water?

The inner road is on BWSSB's Cauvery network. Bommanahalli is one of the eight old zones covered by Cauvery Stage 5, built for villages added to the city in 2007; by May 2026, about 1.15 lakh of an estimated 1.8 lakh buildings in those villages were connected (The Hindu, news). Outside GBA, expect borewells and tankers. Our Cauvery Stage 5 guide explains how to check.

Is Hosur Road flood-prone?

Some junctions are. The GBA's 2026 pre-monsoon circular named Silk Board and Madiwala among the flood-prone locations flagged (The Hindu, news). In April 2026, the South City Corporation commissioner said KAS Officers' Layout near Silk Board was unlikely to flood after a parallel drain was built along the ring road, and ordered desilting below Madiwala lake (The Hindu, news).

Those are road and junction points, not whole areas. Check your street, the nearest lake and drain, and the basement. Our lake and drain checks show how.

Hosur Road buyer's checklist

  • [ ] Confirm GBA or outside, from the PIN, ward and khata.
  • [ ] Find the exact line on Kaveri.
  • [ ] Work out the price-to-guidance ratio.
  • [ ] Walk to the nearest Yellow Line station.
  • [ ] Visit at night to judge highway noise.
  • [ ] Ask for BWSSB bills, or borewell and tanker details outside GBA.
  • [ ] Visit after heavy rain.
  • [ ] Review RERA filings for a new project and get title checked by a lawyer.

What mistakes do buyers make on Hosur Road?

  • Treating it as one market. Floors fall about sevenfold from the inner road to Singasandra's interior.
  • Assuming you're in the city. Past Electronic City, PIN 560099 is outside GBA.
  • Ignoring highway noise. A short walk to the metro can mean a flat on the highway.
  • Using Electronic City figures for the whole road. They don't fit Madiwala or Bommanahalli.

Legal and tax note

This is general information. Ask a CA about income-tax rules on price and guidance value, and a property lawyer about title, khata and approvals before you pay an advance.
