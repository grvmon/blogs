Bellandur sits on the outer ring road between Marathahalli and Sarjapur Road, around the city's largest lake. Buyers come for the tech parks along the ring road. This guide sticks to official records and named sources, and says plainly where data runs out.

Who governs Bellandur?

Bellandur is inside the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) area, so records are e-Khata on e-Aasthi and charges are about 7.6% of value. Lake-heavy pin codes can have large areas outside ward polygons, so use our ward and corporation lookup and confirm from the tax receipt.

What are property rates in Bellandur?

Official guidance values

Residential site rates from the 2023 gazette (in force since 1 Oct 2023), Shivajinagar registration-district book:

Gazette line (residential site) Per sq m Per sq ft
Chikkabellanduru ₹36,000 ₹3,345
Amani Bellandur Khane gramathana ₹48,600 ₹4,515
Amani Bellandur Khane village (listed survey number) ₹77,000 ₹7,153
Bellanduru (village) ₹93,285 ₹8,666
Apartment lines in the Bellanduru section (many projects) ₹63,690 for most, up to ₹81,400 ₹5,917–7,562

Flats are notified project by project, so we don't name them. Look up your own on Kaveri, and compare localities in our rates-by-area table. A revision has been reported in the news, but none had been notified as of 19 Oct 2026.

Market prices

We don't have a sourced Bellandur figure. Knight Frank's 17-location table doesn't include it, and ANAROCK lists Bellandur in its East zone without a rate. The nearest benchmarks are ANAROCK's Q1 2026 base rates of ₹11,400 per sq ft for Sarjapur Road and ₹10,680 for Whitefield, and Knight Frank's ₹7,310 to ₹15,500 for Marathahalli (our Marathahalli guide). For a specific building, check past registered values on Kaveri encumbrance certificates.

Rents

No Bellandur rent is published by ANAROCK. Nearby, it quotes ₹33,000 to ₹46,000 a month for a 1,000 sq ft 2BHK on Sarjapur Road in Q1 2026. Listing-portal rents aren't evidence.

What's being built

Bellandur falls in Bengaluru East taluk on Karnataka RERA. In our East RERA study, the taluk had 1,235 approved housing and plotted projects, and 63.1% still carried their original completion date. These aren't Bellandur-only figures.

Is there a metro in Bellandur?

Not yet.

Line What it does Status (Oct 2026)
Blue Line Phase 2A, Silk Board–KR Puram Runs along the ring road through Bellandur, with a Bellandur station (station names per news reports), linking to the Yellow Line at Silk Board and the Purple Line at KR Puram Targeted for mid-2027, per the Bengaluru development minister (The Hindu, news)
Suburban Corridor-4 (Kanaka) Bellandur is an at-grade station in the southern package K-RIDE reported station raft concrete complete in May 2026; no revised completion date for the corridor

Suburban details are from K-RIDE's May 2026 newsletter and the government's reply to Lok Sabha question 583 of 22 Jul 2026. See every line in our East Bengaluru infrastructure tracker.

Where does Bellandur get its water?

The Bellandur belt falls in the former Mahadevapura zone, one of eight zones covered by Cauvery Stage 5 for villages added to the city in 2007 (JICA). By May 2026, about 1.15 lakh of an estimated 1.8 lakh buildings in those villages were connected (The Hindu, news). Ask for the BWSSB connection details and bills. Our Cauvery Stage 5 guide shows how.

Is Bellandur flood-prone?

Parts of it, yes. In September 2022, the civic body said overflowing Bellandur and Varthur lakes were causing floods in surrounding areas (The Hindu, news), and residents of Yemalur and Marathahalli were told to move out to safety (The New Indian Express, news). In April 2026, the East corporation had 20 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations, and 52 of the 101 citywide were still unfixed (TNIE, news).

Lake buffers became size-based (0 to 30 m) under Act 19 of 2026, and primary drains carry a 50 m buffer under RMP-2015. Our lake and drain checks show how to check a site.

Worked examples

Worked example 1: a new flat at a nearby benchmark

Illustrative. We use ANAROCK's Q1 2026 Sarjapur Road base rate of ₹11,400 per sq ft because no figure exists for this locality. A 1,200 sq ft flat on a ₹63,690 per sq m apartment line.

Item Working Result
Price 1,200 × ₹11,400 ₹1,36,80,000
Guidance value 1,200 ÷ 10.764 × ₹63,690 ₹71,00,334
Price ÷ guidance value ₹1,36,80,000 ÷ ₹71,00,334 1.93
Charges at 7.6% 7.6% × ₹1,36,80,000 ₹10,39,680

Duty goes on the price. Kaveri can add floor, amenity and parking amounts, so check the exact value. Our full cost guide adds GST and other costs.

Worked example 2: an older resale flat priced below the floor

Illustrative. A 1,000 sq ft resale flat on the same line, agreed at ₹5,500 per sq ft.

Item Working Result
Price 1,000 × ₹5,500 ₹55,00,000
Guidance value 1,000 ÷ 10.764 × ₹63,690 ₹59,16,945
Charges at 7.6% on the guidance value 7.6% × ₹59,16,945 ₹4,49,688
Extra vs charges on the price ₹4,49,688 − ₹4,18,000 ₹31,688

The guidance value is within 110% of the price (₹60.5 lakh), so the income-tax gap rules in our guidance value vs market price post shouldn't be triggered. Confirm with your CA.

Worked example 3: a 1,200 sq ft site on three lines

Bellandur buyer's checklist

  • [ ] Ward and city corporation confirmed from the tax receipt or e-Khata
  • [ ] Guidance value line found on Kaveri for the exact flat or site
  • [ ] Past registered values checked on encumbrance certificates
  • [ ] Distance to the lake and nearest drain checked; flood history asked
  • [ ] Water source and BWSSB connection confirmed in writing, with bills
  • [ ] Commute timed without counting the metro
  • [ ] RERA registration, completion date and extensions checked
  • [ ] Title, approvals and occupancy checked by a lawyer (our legal checks guide)

This is general information, not legal or tax advice. Ask a property lawyer to check title and approvals, and a CA about the tax side.
