Hoskote vs Budigere Cross: the data side by side

a&k
acre&key
Acrenkey Realty LLP • 19 Oct 2026 • 10 min read
Share:

Short answer: Hoskote vs Budigere Cross is a comparison with thin official data on both sides. Neither has a Knight Frank or ANAROCK price. Guidance values are close: ₹26,000 per sq m on Hoskote's Old Madras Road line and ₹30,310 in Avalahalli near Budigere Cross. Both are stops on a proposed KR Puram–Hoskote metro.

Key takeaways
  • No consultant price for either: ANAROCK names Budigere Cross as an East micro-market but publishes no rate; portals show asking prices, not registrations.
  • Guidance values: Hoskote town lines run ₹18,000 to ₹30,000 per sq m; villages around Budigere Cross ₹22,000 to ₹32,500.
  • Metro is a plan: BMRCL is preparing a project report for a 16.3 km KR Puram–Hoskote line with stations at Avalahalli, Budigere Cross and Hoskote (news).
  • Outside the city: both sit mostly outside GBA, so stamp charges are 7.65% and water depends on borewells and tankers unless a project proves otherwise.
  • Our EDGE test: entity, drainage, guidance gap, expected transit.

What are property rates in Hoskote and Budigere Cross?

Only official floors are available for both. Hoskote town's lines run from ₹18,000 per sq m on Madras Road East to ₹26,000 on Old Madras Road, with some private layouts at up to ₹30,000.

We couldn't find a gazette row named Budigere Cross. The villages around the junction, in the KR Puram sub-registrar area, are valued at:

Portals publish asking prices for both, often well above these floors. Those aren't registered values, so pull encumbrance certificates for recent sales near the property.

Which has better metro prospects?

They share one plan. BMRCL is preparing a project report for a 16.3 km line from KR Puram to Hoskote with 11 stations, including Avalahalli, Budigere Cross, Hoskote Toll Plaza and Hoskote Government Hospital (Construction World, 8 Oct 2026, news). Station names per news reports. It still needs technical, financial and land approvals, and has no date.

Budigere Cross is closer to the running Purple Line at Whitefield (Kadugodi). Our metro lines guide tracks the rest.

Who runs each area, and why does it matter?

Hoskote town has a City Municipal Council, so records sit on e-Aasthi. Budigere Cross is mostly panchayat land, with records on e-Swathu and layout approvals from BDA or BMRDA depending on the location. Addresses outside GBA pay 7.65% in total stamp charges, against 7.6% inside the city. Our approvals guide shows which authority to check.

Which has better water supply?

Neither is on Cauvery Stage 5, which serves the 110 villages inside the former BBMP limits. A state pre-feasibility study found Hoskote had no surface source and relied on borewells, and AMRUT 2.0 water works were tendered in October 2024. We found no public scheme for the villages around Budigere Cross. Ask every project for its water source in writing; our water questions guide lists what to ask.

Which floods more?

An IISc-led study found nearly 40% of Hoskote taluk's area faces high to very high flood hazard (city news report, 14 Oct 2026, news). We didn't find a comparable figure for Bengaluru East taluk, where Budigere Cross sits. That's taluk-wide, not plot-level. Check lakes, drains and low ground with our lake and drain checks.
