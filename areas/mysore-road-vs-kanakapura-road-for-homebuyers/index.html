Mysore Road vs Kanakapura Road: two roads that already have a metro

Arvind Patidar
Advisory · Execution • Published: 19 Oct 2026 • 10 min read
Share:

Short answer: Mysore Road vs Kanakapura Road is a rare comparison where both roads already have a running metro. The Purple Line has reached Challaghatta on Mysore Road since 2023, and the Green Line has reached Silk Institute on Kanakapura Road since 2021. Benchmarks are close: ANAROCK's ₹7,870 per sq ft for Mysore Road against Knight Frank's ₹7,700 midpoint for Kanakapura Road.

Key takeaways
  • Different trackers: ANAROCK prices Mysore Road but not Kanakapura Road; Knight Frank prices Kanakapura Road but not Mysore Road. Line them up with care.
  • Rent evidence: only Mysore Road has a published 2BHK rent, ₹20,000–26,000 a month, which is about a 3.5% gross yield.
  • Both metros end at the city edge: Challaghatta's site value is ₹23,000 per sq m; Kaggalipura, past Silk Institute, is ₹17,000.
  • Both leave GBA: Kengeri's PIN is about 29% inside GBA, and Kanakapura Road's PIN 560082 is wholly outside.
  • Our TERM test: terminus, edge, rent evidence, main road or interior.

Can you compare their prices directly?

Only roughly. ANAROCK's base rate is one figure for a project-level benchmark (ANAROCK Q1 2026). Knight Frank gives a range across the location (Knight Frank H1 2026). The two methods differ, so a ₹170 gap between ₹7,870 and ₹7,700 tells you the roads sit in a similar band, not that one is cheaper.

The better comparison is the specific flat against recent registrations nearby. Our encumbrance certificate steps show how to find them.

More on this topic sits under Localities & Corridors.

What happens at the end of each line?

This is where the roads differ most. On Mysore Road, the Purple Line runs out to Challaghatta, where sites are valued at ₹23,000 per sq m. Kengeri's PIN 560060 is only about 29% inside GBA.

On Kanakapura Road, the Green Line stops at Silk Institute (PIB). Thalaghattapura, near the terminus, is valued at ₹42,000, and Kaggalipura, further out, at ₹17,000. PIN 560082 is wholly outside GBA.

Past the boundary, charges go from 7.6% to 7.65% and the khata comes from a panchayat. Check your address with our ward and corporation lookup.

Buyers also compare this corridor with Hoskote property rates.

Which floods more?

The corporation counts differ, but they cover far more than these roads. In April 2026, the West City Corporation had 48 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations and the South had 24 (The New Indian Express, news). The report didn't name streets on either road. Mysore Road runs near the Vrishabhavathi valley, so check drains closely; use our lake and drain checks.

Buyers also compare this corridor with Whitefield property rates.

What about water?

Both inner roads are on BWSSB's Cauvery network, and the villages added in 2007 are being connected through Cauvery Stage 5 (The Hindu, news). Past the GBA boundary on either road, expect borewells and tankers. See our Cauvery Stage 5 guide.

Buyers also compare this corridor with Sarjapur Road property rates.
