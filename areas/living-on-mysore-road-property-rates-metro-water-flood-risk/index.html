Living on Mysore Road: property rates, metro, water and flood risk

Market Strategy · Product · Tech • Published: 19 Oct 2026 • 11 min read

Mysore Road runs south-west from the city market area past Goripalya, Nayandahalli, Rajarajeshwari Nagar and Kengeri towards Kumbalgodu and Bidadi. It's an older corridor with industry, layouts and newer apartment pockets, and one of the cheaper ways to live on a running metro line. This guide uses official records and named sources, and says where data runs out.

Who governs Mysore Road?

The inner and middle road is in the West City Corporation. PIN 560026 is 90% West, PIN 560039 (Nayandahalli, Hosakerehalli) is fully West, and PIN 560056 (Jnana Bharathi) is 96% West. Further out it changes: Kengeri's PIN 560060 is only about 29% inside GBA, and PIN 560074 (Kumbalgodu side) is wholly outside.

Inside GBA, charges add up to 7.6%; outside, 7.65%, and the khata comes from a panchayat. Check your plot with our ward and corporation lookup and our guide to BDA, BMRDA, GBA or panchayat approvals.

Market prices and rents

ANAROCK's Q1 2026 report put Mysore Road's base rate at ₹7,870 per sq ft, with 2BHK rents of ₹20,000 to ₹26,000 a month. Both figures were up about 1% to 2% on the quarter. Knight Frank's H1 2026 price table doesn't include Mysore Road. A base rate is a new-launch quote, not a resale price.

What's being built

We don't have a reliable count for the road. Taluk totals from the RERA register are too broad to isolate it, and the register has no locality field. Before you book, use our RERA project review guide.

Is there a metro on Mysore Road?

Yes. The Purple Line runs along Mysore Road. Its full 43.49 km, 37-station route from Whitefield (Kadugodi) to Challaghatta has run since 9 October 2023 (The Hindu, news).

Line Stations along the road (station names per news reports) Status, 19 Oct 2026
Purple Line Mysuru Road, Nayandahalli, Rajarajeshwari Nagar, Jnanabharathi, Pattanagere, Kengeri Bus Terminal, Kengeri, Challaghatta Running

That gives a direct run through the city centre to the east. Our metro lines guide covers the rest.

Where does Mysore Road get its water?

The inner road is on BWSSB's Cauvery network. RR Nagar is one of the eight old zones covered by Cauvery Stage 5, built for villages added to the city in 2007; by May 2026, about 1.15 lakh of an estimated 1.8 lakh buildings in those villages were connected (The Hindu, news). Outside GBA, expect borewells and tankers. Our Cauvery Stage 5 guide explains how to check.

Is Mysore Road flood-prone?

Some points are. In April 2026, the West City Corporation had 48 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations, the most of any corporation, and 52 of the 101 citywide were still unfixed (The New Indian Express, news). The report didn't list streets, and West covers far more than this road.

The road runs near the Vrishabhavathi valley and its drains. Primary drains carry a 50 m buffer under RMP-2015, and lake buffers are size-based (0 to 30 m) under Act 19 of 2026. See our lake and drain checks and flood-prone areas map.

Worked example 2: gross rental yield

A 1,100 sq ft 2BHK at ₹7,870 per sq ft costs ₹86,57,000. At ANAROCK's rent range of ₹20,000 to ₹26,000 a month, gross yield is 2.8% to 3.6% a year, before maintenance, tax and vacancy.

Score each check green, amber or red. Two reds and you should walk away or renegotiate.

Mysore Road buyer's checklist

  • [ ] Confirm GBA or panchayat from the PIN, ward and khata.
  • [ ] Find the exact line on Kaveri.
  • [ ] Work out the price-to-guidance ratio.
  • [ ] Walk to the nearest Purple Line station.
  • [ ] Check distance to the valley drains and visit after rain.
  • [ ] Ask for BWSSB bills, or borewell and tanker details.
  • [ ] Review RERA filings for a new project.
  • [ ] Get title and approvals checked by a property lawyer.

What mistakes do buyers make on Mysore Road?

  • Assuming Kengeri is fully in the city. Most of PIN 560060 is outside GBA.
  • Reading the base rate as a resale price. It's a new-launch figure.
  • Ignoring the valley. Low ground near primary drains needs a rainy-day visit.
  • Skipping the industrial neighbours. Some stretches sit next to industrial areas.

Legal and tax note

This is general information. Ask a CA about income-tax rules on price and guidance value, and a property lawyer about title, khata and approvals before you pay an advance.
