Koramangala property rates (2026): metro, water and flood risk

Gaurav Mongia
Market Strategy · Product · Tech • Published: 19 Oct 2026 • 13 min read
Share:

Short answer: Koramangala property rates aren't published by Knight Frank or ANAROCK, so official guidance values are the firmest guide. Most blocks are ₹1,14,000 per sq m (about ₹10,590 per sq ft), the 3rd Block is ₹1,40,000, and the Hosur–Sarjapur road and inner ring road frontage is ₹1,69,000. No metro line runs through Koramangala yet.

Key takeaways
  • No consultant figure: ANAROCK lists Koramangala in its South zone without a rate; Knight Frank leaves it out of its price table.
  • Blocks share a rate: ₹1,14,000 per sq m for blocks 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and KHB Colony.
  • Main roads cost more: 80 ft roads are ₹1,60,000 and the inner ring road ₹1,69,000.
  • The K-100 drain matters: ST Bed Layout flooded in May 2025 (news), and an undersized inlet to Bellandur lake was flagged in April 2026 (news).
  • Our 4C check: catchment, commercial creep, compliance, cost floor.

Koramangala is a planned BDA area in south-east Bengaluru, laid out in numbered blocks between Hosur Road and the inner ring road. If you are comparing established, high-value central neighbourhoods with vibrant commercial high streets, see our detailed guide to Indiranagar property rates, metro access and flood risk. It mixes older houses, small apartment blocks and a busy commercial core. This guide uses official records and named sources, and says where data runs out.

Who governs Koramangala?

The South City Corporation. PIN 560034 is fully South, with Koramangala East, Koramangala West and Jakkasandra wards, and PIN 560095 is fully South too. Ejipura, in PIN 560047, is split between South and Central. All of it is inside GBA, so stamp duty, cess, surcharge and registration add up to 7.6%. Use our ward and corporation lookup to confirm.

Market prices

We didn't find a consultant price for Koramangala. ANAROCK's Q1 2026 report lists it in the South zone with no rate, and Knight Frank names it only in its zone list and as an office district. Portal figures are asking prices. Ask for registered sale deeds on the same road.

What's being built

Very little large-scale. New homes are mostly rebuilds and small blocks. Under section 3 of the RERA Act, a project is exempt only if its land is 500 sq m or less or it has eight flats or fewer, so check whether a block you're offered should be registered. Our RERA project review guide shows how.

Is there a metro in Koramangala?

Not yet.

Line Near Koramangala (station names per news reports) Status, 19 Oct 2026
Pink Line, underground section Dairy Circle, on the Tavarekere–Nagawara stretch Expected by March 2027, per the minister's earlier statement (news)
Phase 3A Red Line (Sarjapura–Hebbal) Underground from Koramangala 2nd Block, per BMRCL's report Awaiting the Centre's approval
Yellow Line Central Silk Board, BTM Layout Running since Aug 2025

The Pink Line's underground route runs through Dairy Circle, M.G. Road and Shivajinagar (The Hindu, news). BMRCL's February 2026 report puts the Red Line underground from Koramangala 2nd Block (BMRCL DPR). Our metro lines guide tracks every line.

Where does Koramangala get its water?

Koramangala is on BWSSB's Cauvery network as an established city area. Supply still varies by street and building, and many buildings top up with borewells or tankers. Ask for the last three BWSSB bills and the building's tanker spend.

Buyers also compare this corridor with Living on Hosur Road.

Is Koramangala flood-prone?

Parts are, mainly low layouts along the Koramangala Valley (K-100) drain. In May 2025, heavy rain flooded ST Bed Layout in Koramangala, and residents were still clearing debris days later (The Hindu, news).

In April 2026, the South City Corporation commissioner inspected the K-100 drain near Jakkasandra. Officials noted that the new diversion channel into Bellandur lake is 5 to 6 m wide against a 30 m primary drain, and he warned that upstream flooding is likely unless the inlet works in heavy rain (The Hindu, news). Primary drains carry a 50 m buffer under RMP-2015. See our lake and drain checks and flood-prone areas map.

Worked example 2: an old house on a 2,400 sq ft site

A 40 × 60 ft site on a ₹1,14,000 block line has a guidance value of ₹2,54,18,060. Charges on that are ₹19,31,773.

Agreed price Charges on price Extra you pay (on guidance value) 110% of price Result

₹2,25,00,000 ₹17,10,000 ₹2,21,773 ₹2,47,50,000 Guidance value ₹6,68,060 above 110%

At ₹2.25 crore, the gap can create income-tax issues for both buyer and seller. Speak to your CA before you sign.

Worked example 3: the floor on a flat

A 1,500 sq ft flat in a building on a ₹69,100 apartment line has a guidance value of ₹96,29,320. Whatever you agree, your charges can't fall below ₹7,31,828 (7.6% of that). Check the building's own line on Kaveri, plus any add-ons.

What mistakes do buyers make in Koramangala?

  • Assuming one Koramangala rate. Block lines, 60 ft roads, 80 ft roads and the ring road all differ.
  • Agreeing far below the floor. It can raise income-tax issues.
  • Ignoring the drain. Low layouts along K-100 have flooded.
  • Counting on a metro now. No line runs through the area yet.

Legal and tax note

This is general information. Ask a CA about the income-tax effect of a price below guidance value, and a property lawyer about title, plan sanction and khata before you pay an advance. Our legal checks guide lists the documents.
