Living on Sarjapur Road: property rates, metro, water and flood risk

a&k
acre&key
Acrenkey Realty LLP • 19 Oct 2026 • 14 min read
Share:

Short answer: Sarjapur Road property rates for flats run ₹5,700 to ₹11,300 per sq ft (Knight Frank, H1 2026), up just 3% in a year. Official guidance values fall steeply along the road, from ₹1,69,000 per sq m at Koramangala to ₹20,000 in Sarjapura village. There's no metro on the road yet, and water and approvals change once you leave the city.

  • Prices cooled: Knight Frank's +3% was the 15th of 17 Bengaluru locations in its table. ANAROCK's Q1 2026 base rate was ₹11,400 per sq ft.
  • Metro is years away: the Sarjapura–Hebbal Red Line (37.8 km, 27 stations) still awaits central approval.

Sarjapur Road runs south-east from Koramangala, past the outer ring road, to Sarjapura town. People say "Sarjapur Road" for all of it, but the numbers that matter change every few kilometres. This guide sticks to what official records and named sources say.

Who governs Sarjapur Road?

The stretch from Koramangala to beyond the outer ring road lies inside the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) area, split across the new city corporations. Further out, much of the road lies in Anekal taluk, outside GBA, where villages are run by gram panchayats and property records sit on e-Swathu rather than e-Aasthi.

That changes three things you'll pay for or depend on:

  1. Records: e-Khata on e-Aasthi in the city; Form 9 or 11 on e-Swathu in panchayats.
  2. Surcharge on stamp duty: 2% of duty in municipal areas, 3% in panchayat areas.
  3. Approvals: BDA or GBA in the city; BMRDA, the local planning authority and the panchayat outside. Our approvals guide shows which to check.

Not sure which side a building is on? Use our ward and corporation lookup, then confirm from the tax receipt.

Flats are notified project by project, so look up your own on Kaveri. Our rates-by-area table has more localities. No revision had been notified as of 19 Oct 2026.

Market prices

Knight Frank's H1 2026 report puts Sarjapur Road flat prices at ₹5,700 to ₹11,300 per sq ft, up 3% over 12 months, 15th of the 17 Bengaluru locations in its table. ANAROCK's Q1 2026 quoted base rate was ₹11,400 per sq ft. Both are consultant figures for new stock, not registered sales. For a specific building, check past registered values on Kaveri encumbrance certificates.

Rents

ANAROCK quotes ₹33,000 to ₹46,000 a month for a 1,000 sq ft 2BHK in Q1 2026, up 1% on the quarter. That's a gross yield of about 4.2% at its base rate. Rents rose fast earlier: CNBC TV18, citing ANAROCK, reported a 76% rise on Sarjapur Road from end-2021 to end-2024 (news).

What's being built

Much of the outer road falls in Anekal taluk on Karnataka RERA. In our East RERA study, Anekal had 874 approved housing and plotted projects, and 72.2% still carried their original completion date. But Anekal also covers Electronic City and Chandapura, so it isn't a Sarjapur-only figure.

Is there a metro on Sarjapur Road?

Not yet. BMRCL's February 2026 project report for Phase 3A plans a 37.804 km line from Sarjapura to Hebbal with 27 stations. It runs elevated along the median of Sarjapura Main Road to Ibbalur junction, then along the outer ring road to Agara, and underground from Koramangala 2nd Block through the city centre (BMRCL DPR, Feb 2026).

As of October 2026 it still awaits the Centre's approval, and BMRCL has started soil testing on Sarjapur Road (The Hindu, 15 Oct 2026, news). Deccan Herald reported in July 2026 that it may open only by 2033 (news).

Closer in, the Blue Line along the outer ring road crosses Sarjapur Road near Ibbalur. BMRCL is reported to be aiming to open the Central Silk Board–KR Puram section in May 2027 (BLR Today, news).

Where does Sarjapur Road get its water?

It depends which side of the city boundary you're on. Cauvery Stage 5 serves the 110 villages added to the city in 2007; by May 2026 about 1.15 lakh of an estimated 1.8 lakh buildings there were connected (The Hindu, news). Parts of the inner Sarjapur Road stretch fall in those former BBMP zones. The Anekal stretch, outside GBA, isn't part of Stage 5, so expect borewells and tankers unless a project shows otherwise.

Our Cauvery Stage 5 guide shows how to check one building.

Is Sarjapur Road flood-prone?

Parts are. The inner road passes lakes and drains in the Bellandur catchment. Flood risk in Bengaluru is tracked as points: in April 2026, 52 of 101 listed flood-prone locations across the five city corporations were still unfixed; of the 101, 24 were in the South corporation and 20 in the East (The New Indian Express, news). Outside GBA, there's no equivalent public list.

Lake buffers became size-based (0 to 30 m) under Act 19 of 2026, and primary drains carry a 50 m buffer under RMP-2015. Our lake and drain checks show how to check a site.

Worked examples

Worked example 1: city or panchayat, same flat

Illustrative. A 1,000 sq ft flat at ANAROCK's base rate of ₹11,400 per sq ft = ₹1,14,00,000.

Charge Inside GBA (2% surcharge) Panchayat area (3% surcharge)
Stamp duty 5% ₹5,70,000 ₹5,70,000
Cess 10% of duty ₹57,000 ₹57,000
Surcharge on duty ₹11,400 ₹17,100
Registration 2% ₹2,28,000 ₹2,28,000
Total ₹8,66,400 ₹8,72,100

The difference is only ₹5,700. The bigger differences between city and panchayat are records, water and approvals. Our full cost guide adds GST and other costs.

  • [ ] GBA or panchayat confirmed from the tax receipt
  • [ ] e-Khata (city) or e-Swathu Form 9/11 (panchayat) matches the deed
  • [ ] Layout or building approval from the right authority
  • [ ] Guidance value line found on Kaveri for the exact property
  • [ ] Past registered values checked on encumbrance certificates
  • [ ] Water source confirmed in writing
  • [ ] Distance to the nearest lake and drain checked; visited after heavy rain
  • [ ] RERA registration, completion date and extensions checked
  • [ ] Commute timed at peak hour, without counting the planned metro
  • [ ] Title and approvals checked by a lawyer (our legal checks guide)

What mistakes do buyers make on Sarjapur Road?

  • Pricing the whole road the same. A Doddakannelli main-road site and a Sarjapura village site are different markets.
  • Buying on the metro. The Red Line hasn't been approved yet.
  • Assuming city water. Outside GBA, Stage 5 doesn't apply.
  • Skipping the boundary check. It decides records, approvals and surcharge.

This is general information, not legal or tax advice. Ask a property lawyer to check title and approvals, and a CA about the tax side.
