Hebbal vs Thanisandra: the data side by side

a&k
acre&key
Acrenkey Realty LLP • 19 Oct 2026 • 11 min read
Share:

Short answer: Hebbal vs Thanisandra comes down to price against timing. Knight Frank put Hebbal at ₹7,800 to ₹15,000 per sq ft in H1 2026, up 11%, and Thanisandra at ₹5,700 to ₹13,500, up 6%. Neither has a running metro. Hebbal's airport line is targeted for 2027; Thanisandra waits on Nagawara.

Key takeaways
  • Price: Hebbal's range sits ₹2,100 to ₹1,500 per sq ft above Thanisandra's at each end (Knight Frank, H1 2026).
  • Growth: Hebbal rose 11% in a year, Thanisandra 6%.
  • Metro: both are waiting. Hebbal–airport is targeted for September 2027; the KR Puram–Hebbal stretch through Nagawara for December 2027 to March 2028 (news).
  • Rent evidence: only Thanisandra has a consultant rent figure, ₹28,000 to ₹42,000 for a 2BHK (ANAROCK).
  • Our SWAP test: spread, when the metro arrives, agreements for rent, pockets that flood.

Both areas sit in north Bengaluru, a few kilometres apart, and both are in the North City Corporation. This comparison reuses the figures in our Hebbal guide and Thanisandra guide, and says where data runs out.

Guidance values are the 2023 rates still in force. Check Kaveri for your registration date; our guidance value table explains why.

Which is cheaper, Hebbal or Thanisandra?

Thanisandra, on Knight Frank's figures. Its range starts ₹2,100 per sq ft lower and tops out ₹1,500 lower. The midpoints are ₹11,400 for Hebbal and ₹9,600 for Thanisandra (Knight Frank).

The official floors are closer. Hebbala's village line is ₹68,200 per sq m against ₹65,000 for Thanisandra. Main-road lines differ more: Hebbal's ring-road line is ₹1,22,200, Thanisandra Main Road ₹78,000.

Which has better metro access?

Neither has a running station today. Both depend on the Blue Line:

Line Hebbal Thanisandra
Blue Line, Hebbal–airport Hebbal station, target September 2027 Not on this stretch
Blue Line, KR Puram–Hebbal Hebbal end Nagawara station, target December 2027 to March 2028
Pink Line, to Nagawara (underground) Not on this line Nagawara terminus, delayed, no firm date
Red Line (Phase 3A), Sarjapura–Hebbal Hebbal terminus, awaiting central approval Not on this line

Station names per news reports. The dates come from the Times of India on 9 Oct 2026 (TOI, news). Deccan Herald reported in August 2026 that work beyond Yelahanka was lagging (Deccan Herald, news). The Red Line route is from BMRCL's February 2026 project report. Our metro lines guide tracks them all.

Which has better rental evidence?

Thanisandra. ANAROCK quotes ₹28,000 to ₹42,000 a month for a 1,000 sq ft 2BHK on Thanisandra Main Road in Q1 2026 (ANAROCK). ANAROCK's table doesn't cover Hebbal, and we don't quote listing-site rents. If rent matters to you, ask for signed lease agreements in the building.

Which floods more?

We can't rank them from public data. In April 2026, the North corporation, which covers both, had 6 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations (The New Indian Express, news). The pockets to check are low streets near Hebbal lake and its valley drains, and near Rachenahalli lake in Thanisandra. See our lake and drain checks and flood-prone areas map.

What about water supply?

The villages around both were added to the city in 2007 and sit in zones covered by Cauvery Stage 5. By May 2026, about 1.15 lakh of an estimated 1.8 lakh buildings in those villages were connected (The Hindu, news). Check the building, not the area: our Cauvery Stage 5 guide shows how.
