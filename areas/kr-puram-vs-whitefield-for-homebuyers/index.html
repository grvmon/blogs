KR Puram vs Whitefield: the data side by side

a&k
acre&key
Acrenkey Realty LLP • 19 Oct 2026 • 11 min read
Share:

Short answer: KR Puram vs Whitefield is a choice between faster recent growth and deeper evidence. Knight Frank put KR Puram at ₹6,400 to ₹13,400 per sq ft in H1 2026, up 20%, and Whitefield at ₹7,100 to ₹15,200, up 14%. Both are on the Purple Line. Only Whitefield has a consultant rent figure.

Key takeaways
  • Price: Whitefield's range sits ₹700 to ₹1,800 per sq ft above KR Puram's at each end (Knight Frank, H1 2026).
  • Growth: KR Puram rose 20% in a year, the fastest of 17 Bengaluru locations; Whitefield rose 14%.
  • Metro: both are on the Purple Line now. KR Puram will also be the Blue Line interchange, with the Silk Board stretch targeted for May 2027 (news).
  • Rent: ANAROCK quotes ₹30,000 to ₹45,000 for a Whitefield 2BHK, about 4.2% gross. There's no consultant figure for KR Puram.
  • Our LINK test: line, income evidence, neighbourhood floor, kerb-level flooding.

Which is cheaper, KR Puram or Whitefield?

KR Puram, on Knight Frank's figures. Its range starts ₹700 per sq ft lower and tops out ₹1,800 lower. The midpoints are ₹9,900 for KR Puram and ₹11,150 for Whitefield (Knight Frank).

The official floors overlap. KR Puram's Hoodi–KR Puram Main Road line is ₹75,000 per sq m, the same as Whitefield's BDA layout line. Whitefield Main Road is ₹80,000; Kodigehalli village near KR Puram is ₹40,000.

Which has better metro access?

Both have the Purple Line today. Its Whitefield (Kadugodi) to KR Puram stretch opened in March 2023, and the full line has run to Challaghatta since 9 October 2023 (The Hindu, news).

KR Puram gets a second line. It's set to be the Blue Line's interchange, and BMRCL is reported to be aiming to open the Central Silk Board–KR Puram section in May 2027 (BLR Today, news). The KR Puram–Hebbal stretch is expected from December 2027 to March 2028 (TOI, news). Station names per news reports. Our metro lines guide tracks both.

Which has better rental evidence?

Whitefield. ANAROCK quotes ₹30,000 to ₹45,000 a month for a 1,000 sq ft 2BHK in Q1 2026, up 1% on the quarter (ANAROCK). We didn't find a named-consultant rent figure for KR Puram, so we don't quote one. Ask for signed leases in the building.

Which floods more?

We can't rank them from public data. Both are in the East corporation, which had 20 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations in April 2026 (The New Indian Express, news). Neither has a public street list. Check low streets, lakes and drains near the building; see our lake and drain checks and flood-prone areas map.

What about water supply?

Both depend on Cauvery Stage 5's Mahadevapura zone for the villages added in 2007. By May 2026, about 1.15 lakh of an estimated 1.8 lakh buildings in those villages were connected (The Hindu, news). Older parts of KR Puram were in the city before 2007. Check the building's own connection; our Cauvery Stage 5 guide shows how.
