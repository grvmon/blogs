Old Madras Road vs Hoskote: reading the road station by station

a&k
acre&key
Acrenkey Realty LLP • 19 Oct 2026 • 10 min read
Share:

Short answer: Old Madras Road vs Hoskote isn't really two places but one road. Official site values fall as you go east: ₹75,000 per sq m on the TC Palya Main Road line in Bhattarahalli, ₹60,000 on the highway at Medahalli, then ₹26,000 on Hoskote's Old Madras Road line. A planned metro would run the whole way.

Key takeaways
  • One road, a falling floor: guidance values drop from ₹75,000 to ₹18,000 per sq m between Bhattarahalli and Hoskote town.
  • Prices are thin at both ends: Knight Frank lists Old Madras Road in its East zone but gives no price; it has none for Hoskote either.
  • Planned metro: BMRCL is preparing a project report for a 16.3 km KR Puram–Hoskote line with 11 stations (news). Nothing runs east of KR Puram yet.
  • Rules change on the way: the inner stretch was in the former BBMP; the outer villages are under panchayats; Hoskote has a municipal council.
  • Our EAST test: entity, access, slope, travel time.

What do market prices look like?

There's little to go on. Knight Frank names Old Madras Road in its East zone but publishes no price for it (H1 2026). Its nearest tracked location is KR Puram, at the corridor's inner end: ₹6,400–13,400 per sq ft, up 20% in a year. Neither consultant gives a Hoskote price.

Portals and builder pages quote prices by stretch, but those are asking prices. Pull encumbrance certificates for recent sales near the property; our encumbrance certificate steps show how.

Who governs each stretch?

Three regimes along one road:

  • Inner stretch (Bhattarahalli, Medahalli): gazette rows here cite former BBMP numbers, so expect e-Khata on e-Aasthi and charges of 7.6%. Confirm from the tax receipt.
  • Outer villages (Kattamnallur, Avalahalli): mostly gram panchayats, with records on e-Swathu and charges of 7.65%. Our Form 11A and 11B page explains panchayat khatas.
  • Hoskote town: Hosakote City Municipal Council, outside GBA, at 7.65%.

Layout approvals can come from BDA, BMRDA or a local planning authority depending on the village. Our approvals guide shows what to check.

What's running, and what's planned?

The Purple Line ends at KR Puram on this side and at Whitefield (Kadugodi) further south. East of KR Puram, the 16.3 km line to Hoskote is at the project-report stage and still needs technical, financial and land approvals (Construction World, 8 Oct 2026, news). At Hoskote, the Satellite Town Ring Road's Dobaspet–Hoskote section is open. Our East Bengaluru infrastructure tracker follows each project.

What about water and floods?

Inner villages added to the city in 2007 fall under Cauvery Stage 5. Outer panchayat villages and Hoskote don't; Hoskote relies largely on borewells, with AMRUT 2.0 works tendered in 2024. An IISc-led study rated nearly 40% of Hoskote taluk as high to very high flood hazard (city news report, 14 Oct 2026, news). Ask every project for its water source in writing; our water questions guide has the list.

From the highway at Medahalli to Hoskote's Old Madras Road line, the floor falls by ₹34,000 per sq m across a 16.3 km line, roughly ₹2,086 per sq m per km. That's an average; the real drop comes in steps.

Worked example 2: when the price is below the floor

Say a seller agrees ₹25,00,000 for a 1,200 sq ft site on Hoskote's Old Madras Road line (illustrative).

Step Working Result
Guidance value 1,200 ÷ 10.764 × ₹26,000 ₹28,98,551
110% of the price ₹25,00,000 × 110% ₹27,50,000
Over the 110% limit? ₹28,98,551 vs ₹27,50,000 Yes
Gap between guidance value and price ₹28,98,551 − ₹25,00,000 ₹3,98,551
Charges at 7.65% on the guidance value 7.65% × ₹28,98,551 ₹2,21,739
Charges if the price were used 7.65% × ₹25,00,000 ₹1,91,250

You pay charges on the guidance value either way. And because it's more than 110% of the price, the gap can be treated as income for you as the buyer under income-tax rules. Ask your CA before you sign.
