Electronic City vs Sarjapur Road: the data side by side

Arvind Patidar
Advisory · Execution • Published: 19 Oct 2026 • 11 min read
Share:

Short answer: Electronic City vs Sarjapur Road is a choice between a running metro now and a higher-priced road waiting for one. Knight Frank put Electronic City at ₹5,700 to ₹8,600 per sq ft in H1 2026, up 10%, and Sarjapur Road at ₹5,700 to ₹11,300, up 3%. Gross yields are similar, at about 4.2%.

Key takeaways
  • Price: the ranges start at the same ₹5,700, but Sarjapur Road's top end is ₹2,700 higher (Knight Frank, H1 2026).
  • Growth: Electronic City rose 10% in a year, Sarjapur Road 3%.
  • Metro: the Yellow Line has run through Electronic City since August 2025. Sarjapur Road's Red Line still awaits central approval.
  • Rent: ANAROCK's 2BHK ranges are ₹20,000 to ₹31,000 and ₹33,000 to ₹46,000, both about 4.2% gross at its base rates.
  • Our JOBS test: job hub, operating metro, boundary, spend.

Both corridors run south-east from the city into Anekal taluk, and both change character once they leave GBA. This comparison reuses the figures in our Electronic City guide and Sarjapur Road guide, and says where data runs out.

Which is cheaper, Electronic City or Sarjapur Road?

Electronic City, on both consultants' figures. Knight Frank's midpoints are ₹7,150 per sq ft for Electronic City and ₹8,500 for Sarjapur Road (Knight Frank). ANAROCK's base rates are further apart, at ₹7,200 and ₹11,400 (ANAROCK).

Sarjapur Road's guidance values swing far more. The gazette values a site near Koramangala at ₹1,69,000 per sq m and one in Sarjapura village at ₹20,000. Electronic City's site lines sit in a narrower ₹37,200 to ₹52,800 band.

Buyers also compare this corridor with Devanahalli vs Yelahanka.

Which has better metro access?

Electronic City. The Yellow Line from RV Road to Bommasandra opened in August 2025 and stops at Electronic City (PIB).

Sarjapur Road has no line yet. The Blue Line along the outer ring road crosses it near Ibbalur, and BMRCL is reported to be aiming to open the Central Silk Board–KR Puram section in May 2027 (BLR Today, news). The Sarjapura–Hebbal Red Line still awaits central approval, though BMRCL has started soil testing (The Hindu, news). Our metro lines guide tracks both.

Buyers also compare this corridor with KR Puram vs Whitefield.

Which gives better rental returns?

They're close. At ANAROCK's base rates and the midpoint of its rent ranges, a 1,000 sq ft 2BHK yields about 4.25% gross in Electronic City and 4.16% on Sarjapur Road. Sarjapur Road earns more rent in rupees but costs more to buy. Both ANAROCK rent figures rose 1% on the quarter.

Buyers also compare this corridor with Hoskote vs Budigere Cross.

Which floods more?

We can't rank them from public data. The April 2026 city list of 101 flood-prone points covers only GBA's five corporations: 24 in the South and 20 in the East (The New Indian Express, news). It doesn't cover the ELCITA township or panchayat areas. Sarjapur Road's inner stretch passes lakes and drains in the Bellandur catchment. See our lake and drain checks and flood-prone areas map.

What about water and approvals?

Both change at the GBA line. Inside, BWSSB supply and e-Khata apply; outside, panchayat records (e-Swathu), borewells and tankers are common, and the stamp duty surcharge is 3% of duty instead of 2%. In Electronic City, the ELCITA township issues its own building licences and khata. Our approvals guide shows which authority applies.
