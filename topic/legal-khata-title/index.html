A-Khata vs B-Khata vs e-Khata: what's the difference in Bengaluru?

In A-Khata vs B-Khata vs e-Khata, the first two are registers and the third is a format. A-Khata records a property that complies with approvals; B-Khata records one with approval or bye-law gaps. An e-Khata is the digital record of either, draft or final. Registration needs an e-Khata, and B-to-A conversion now costs 5% of guidance value.

DC conversion in Karnataka and Section 95 explained for plot buyers

DC conversion in Karnataka is permission under Section 95 of the Karnataka Land Revenue Act to use farm land for other purposes. Since 23 Dec 2025, land inside an approved master plan needs no Deputy Commissioner's permission. Elsewhere, the DC must decide within 30 days or approval is deemed. Either way, check the order against the RTC.
