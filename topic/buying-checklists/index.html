Carpet area and loading calculator for Bengaluru flat buyers

Loading is the super built-up area minus the RERA carpet area, divided by the carpet area. A flat quoted at 1,350 sq ft with a 1,000 sq ft carpet area has 35% loading, so you pay 1.35 times the quoted rate for each square foot you can use. RERA sets no loading limit. It defines carpet area and makes builders disclose it.
