Flood-prone areas in Bengaluru (2026): what official data shows and how to check a flat

Flood-prone areas in Bengaluru are tracked as points, not neighbourhoods. In April 2026, the five city corporations had 101 flood-prone locations (48 West, 24 South, 20 East, 6 North, 3 Central), and 52 were still unfixed. Before buying, check the street, the nearest lake and drain buffers, and the site after heavy rain.

Buying near a lake in Bengaluru: is it safe near a lake or rajakaluve?

Buying near a lake in Bengaluru can be fine, but only after three checks on the specific property: its distance from the lake's revenue boundary or the drain, the survey map that proves where that boundary is, and the date the plan was approved. Lake buffers became size-based on 18 Feb 2026, and drain encroachments are still being cleared.

In May 2024, before the monsoon, the Central Ground Water Board (CGWB) measured water at a median depth of 5.93 m below ground across 29 monitoring wells in the Bangalore City block, and 17.61 m across 31 wells in the rest of Bengaluru Urban and Bengaluru Rural.
