Bengaluru metro new lines 2026: the status of every line for homebuyers

Of the Bengaluru metro new lines in 2026, only the Yellow Line (opened August 2025) is running, alongside the Purple and Green Lines, for about 96 km in all. The Pink Line's 7.5 km elevated stretch awaits a safety clearance. The Blue Line to the airport is 72.5% built. The Orange and Red Lines are years away.

Namma Metro Phase 3: the route, the stations and the areas likely to gain access

The Namma Metro Phase 3 route is two elevated corridors, together called the Orange Line. One runs 32.15 km from JP Nagar 4th Phase to Kempapura along the western outer ring road, with 22 stations. The other runs 12.50 km from Hosahalli to Kadabagere on Magadi Road, with 9. It was approved in 2024 with a 2029 target.

East Bengaluru infrastructure projects: where does each one stand in October 2026?

Of the big east Bengaluru infrastructure projects, only the Purple Line metro and the STRR's Dobaspet–Hoskote section are running. The Blue Line's Silk Board–KR Puram stretch is reportedly targeting May 2027. Suburban rail to Whitefield is still acquiring land. PRR-1's eastern packages aren't tendered, and the KR Puram–Hoskote metro is only a project report.

15 questions before buying near a metro station

Before buying near a metro station in Bengaluru, check the line's real stage (running, under construction or only proposed), walk the route to the station gate, and listen for noise from the viaduct. Ask how long construction will block your road, and what markup you're paying. Dates slip: the Blue Line's full opening is now reported for March 2028.

Metro TOD zones in Bengaluru: what extra FAR means for buyers

Search for metro TOD zones Bengaluru and you'll find ads, not notified plans: we found none on 19 October 2026. What exists since February 2026 is citywide. Builders can buy up to 0.6 times the normal FAR on roads 9 m or wider. For you, that means taller buildings, more flats per plot and a smaller land share.

Bengaluru suburban rail stations: nearby areas for homebuyers

Bengaluru suburban rail stations are being built on two of the four corridors. K-RIDE has named 12 stations on Corridor-2 (Mallige) and 18 on Corridor-4 (Kanaka) in its station contracts. None is open. Reported targets are December 2028 for Mallige, June 2029 for Kanaka and March 2030 for the other two.
