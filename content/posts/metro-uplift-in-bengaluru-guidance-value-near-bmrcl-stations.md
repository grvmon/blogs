# Metro uplift in Bengaluru: what guidance values near stations show

**Short answer:** The metro uplift in Bengaluru shows up in guidance values only as a main-road markup. On Mysore Road, plots attached to the road carry ₹1,30,000 per sq m against ₹55,000 on Goripalya Main Road. But Old Madras Road, with no metro, shows a 28% frontage markup too, and the 2023 rates predate the Yellow Line.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **Big gaps on metro roads:** 2.4 times on Mysore Road at Goripalya, 1.8 times on Tumkur Road, in the 2023 guidance values.
> - **Frontage does much of the work:** Old Madras Road at Medahalli, with no running metro, still carries a 28% highway markup.
> - **Metro roads aren't always top:** in Rajajinagar, Dr Rajkumar Road (Green Line) is valued below the Bashyam Circle area.
> - **The 2023 rates can't see newer lines:** they took effect on 1 Oct 2023, before the full Purple Line, the Green Line extension and the Yellow Line.
> - **Our STOP test** helps you decide whether a metro uplift in the price is worth paying.

Guidance value (GV) is the state's minimum value for registration. It's set by road and area, not by distance to a station, so there's no "metro column" in the gazette. Our [guidance value table](https://acrenkey.com/insights/bengaluru-property-rates-by-area-guidance-value-table-2026/) and [GV vs market price guide](https://acrenkey.com/insights/guidance-value-vs-market-price-east-bengaluru/) explain how the rates work. What you can do is compare rows on a metro road with rows just off it.

## Is that a metro uplift or a main-road markup?

Mostly you can't tell, and that's the honest finding.

- **Old Madras Road** shows a 28% gap between the highway and an interior main road in the same village, with no metro running. That's a pure frontage markup. See our [Old Madras Road vs Hoskote comparison](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/old-madras-road-vs-hoskote-for-homebuyers/) for the full gradient.
- **Rajajinagar** shows the metro road valued 14% below a commercial circle nearby. Commercial activity beats the metro there.
- **Mysore Road and Tumkur Road** show the biggest gaps, but they're highways with commercial frontage as well as metro lines.

So a big GV gap on a metro road tells you the road is valuable. It doesn't tell you how much of that comes from the metro.

## Do the 2023 rates include newer metro lines?

No. The current rates took effect on 1 Oct 2023. Since then:

| Line or stretch | Opened | In 2023 GV? |
|---|---|---|
| Purple Line, full route to Whitefield and Challaghatta | 9 Oct 2023 ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/bangalore/bengaluru-operations-on-entire-whitefield-challaghatta-purple-line-metro-corridor-to-start-on-october-9/article67397106.ece), news) | No, opened 8 days later |
| Green Line extension to Madavara | Nov 2024 ([The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/karnataka/bengalurus-namma-metro-green-line-extends-to-madavara), news) | No |
| Yellow Line, RV Road to Bommasandra | 10 Aug 2025 ([PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2154848)) | No |
| Pink Line, elevated stretch | Not yet open | No |

Our position: the 2023 rates are still in force. A revision of 12% to 15% was reported as being prepared in February 2026 ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/karnataka/2026/Feb/21/guidance-value-yet-to-be-hiked-by-15-across-karnataka-expect-property-rates-to-go-up), news). Check the current value on [Kaveri](https://kaveri.karnataka.gov.in/) before you register. Our [metro lines status page](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-metro-lines-2026-status-of-every-line-for-homebuyers/) tracks each line.

## Worked examples

### Worked example 1: the Goripalya gradient on a 1,200 sq ft site

| Row | ₹/sq ft | GV of a 1,200 sq ft site | Stamp charges at 7.6% on GV |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attached to Mysore Main Road | 12,077 | ₹1,44,92,400 | ₹11,01,422 |
| Goripalya Main Road | 5,110 | ₹61,32,000 | ₹4,66,032 |
| Goripalya Cross Road | 4,181 | ₹50,17,200 | ₹3,81,307 |

Moving one row back from the metro road cuts the GV floor by more than half. If you're quoted a road-facing price for a plot that the gazette puts on the main road inside Goripalya, that's a question to ask.

### Worked example 2: what a metro uplift costs in duty

You're paying ₹90,00,000 for a flat near a Yellow Line station (see our [Hosur Road guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/living-on-hosur-road-property-rates-metro-water-flood-risk/)), and a similar flat 2 km away sells for ₹80,00,000. Duty is charged on the higher of price and GV, so on the price here:

| | Near station | 2 km away | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | ₹90,00,000 | ₹80,00,000 | ₹10,00,000 |
| Stamp charges at 7.6% | ₹6,84,000 | ₹6,08,000 | ₹76,000 |
| Total outlay | ₹96,84,000 | ₹86,08,000 | ₹10,76,000 |

The extra ₹10 lakh costs you ₹10.76 lakh, not ₹10 lakh. And because the 2023 GV doesn't reflect the Yellow Line, the GV won't support that extra on paper. Run your own numbers with our [stamp duty calculator](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/stamp-duty-and-registration-calculator-karnataka/).

## The STOP test: is a metro uplift worth paying? (our framework)

| Letter | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| S: Status | Is the line running, or still under construction or proposed ([Phase 3 routes](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/namma-metro-phase-3-route-areas-likely-to-gain-access/))? | A running line is a fact; a planned one is a forecast. |
| T: Time to walk | How many minutes on foot to the station, by a real route? | A station "nearby" across a highway may be a 20-minute walk. |
| O: On the road | Are you paying for metro access or for main-road frontage, noise and dust? | GV shows frontage carries a markup of its own. |
| P: Price vs GV | How far is the price above the GV for this row? | A wide gap means the extra is market belief, not a state floor. |

## Checklist before paying a metro uplift

- [ ] Confirm the line's status on BMRCL's site or our status page.
- [ ] Walk from the gate to the station at peak hour.
- [ ] Look up the GV row for the exact road on Kaveri.
- [ ] Compare the price with a similar flat one road back.
- [ ] Check noise and dust if the flat faces the viaduct.
- [ ] Ask whether parking or lift access changes near the station.
- [ ] Add stamp charges to the extra you're paying.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does guidance value go up near a metro station?

Not directly. GV is set by road and area, not station distance. Metro roads often carry high GV, but frontage and commercial use explain much of it.

### What is the metro uplift in Bengaluru?

There's no official figure. Our 2023 GV comparison shows main-road rows at 1.25 to 2.9 times nearby side roads, but that mixes frontage with metro access.

### Does the 2023 guidance value include the Yellow Line?

No. The 2023 rates took effect on 1 Oct 2023, and the Yellow Line opened on 10 Aug 2025. Check Kaveri for the current value before registering.

### Is guidance value being revised in 2026?

A 12% to 15% revision was reported as being prepared in February 2026 (news). Our position is that the 2023 rates remain in force; check Kaveri.

### Is a flat facing the metro better than one a road back?

Not always. A road-facing flat gets access but also noise and dust. GV often values the road row higher, but you may prefer one road back.

### How do I find the GV for my road?

Search the Kaveri portal by district, area and road, or ask the sub-registrar office. Use the exact row, as values change from one road to the next.

### Do I pay stamp duty on the metro uplift?

Yes. Duty is charged on the higher of the price and the GV. A ₹10 lakh extra adds ₹76,000 at 7.6%, so it costs ₹10.76 lakh in total.

### Should I buy near a proposed metro line?

Treat it as a forecast. Proposed and under-construction lines have slipped before; see the Phase 3 routes for what's planned. Pay for what's running and treat the rest as possible upside.
