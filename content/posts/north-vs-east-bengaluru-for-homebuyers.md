# North vs East Bengaluru: what the data says

**Short answer:** North vs East Bengaluru splits on momentum and jobs. In H1 2026, Knight Frank found North's sales up 11% and East's down 9%. East's locations grew faster on price, though, and East has the jobs: 29.3% of office leasing against 3.2% for North. East has a running metro; North waits for the Blue Line.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **Sales:** North took 31% of city sales in H1 2026 and East 29%, a reversal from 30% and 34% a year earlier (Knight Frank).
> - **Prices:** Knight Frank's East locations rose 14–20% in 12 months; its North locations 6–18%.
> - **Stock:** East had 22,878 unsold homes (4.9 quarters to sell); North 15,408 (3.9 quarters), up 34% in a year.
> - **Jobs:** the Peripheral Business District East took 29.3% of office leasing; North took 3.2%.
> - **Our DESK test:** demand, employment, stock, kilometres of running rail.

"North" and "East" mean different things on different sites. This page uses Knight Frank's zones. North is Hebbal, Bellary Road, Hennur, Jakkur, Yelahanka and Banaswadi. East is Whitefield, Old Airport Road, Old Madras Road, KR Puram and Marathahalli.

## How do North and East Bengaluru compare?

| Measure, H1 2026 | North | East |
|---|---|---|
| Share of launches (H1 2025) | 29% (27%) | 31% (32%) |
| Share of sales (H1 2025) | 31% (30%) | 29% (34%) |
| Sales change, year on year | +11% | −9% |
| Unsold homes (YoY change) | 15,408 (+34%) | 22,878 (+19%) |
| Quarters to sell | 3.9 | 4.9 |
| Locations Knight Frank tracks | Hebbal, Yelahanka, Thanisandra, Hennur | KR Puram, Whitefield, Marathahalli |
| 12-month price change, by location | 6% to 18% | 14% to 20% |
| Share of office leasing | 3.2% (PBD North) | 29.3% (PBD East) |
| Metro today | No running line in these locations | Purple Line to Whitefield |

All figures from [Knight Frank's H1 2026 report](https://content.knightfrank.com/research/3116/documents/en/india-real-estate-office-and-residential-market-h1-2026-12927.pdf). PBD is Knight Frank's Peripheral Business District; PBD North covers Thanisandra, Yelahanka and Devanahalli.

## Which zone is selling faster?

North. Knight Frank calls it the city's fastest-growing residential corridor, with launches up 10% and sales up 11%. East's sales fell 9% as buyers worked through earlier supply.

The city sold 27,968 homes in H1 2026 ([ET Realty](https://realty.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/residential/bengaluru-housing-market-sees-5-sales-increase-in-h1-2026/132356572), news, citing Knight Frank). At the zone shares, that's about 8,670 in North and 8,110 in East.

## Where have prices risen more?

East, by location. Knight Frank's 12-month changes:

| Zone | Location | ₹/sq ft, H1 2026 | 12 months | 6 months |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| East | KR Puram | 6,400–13,400 | 20% | 7% |
| East | Marathahalli | 7,310–15,500 | 15% | 3% |
| East | Whitefield | 7,100–15,200 | 14% | 9% |
| North | Yelahanka | 6,300–12,700 | 18% | 15% |
| North | Hebbal | 7,800–15,000 | 11% | 16% |
| North | Hennur | 7,000–14,000 | 10% | 18% |
| North | Thanisandra | 5,700–13,500 | 6% | 3% |

North's six-month figures are stronger in three of four locations, so its prices may be catching up. ## Which zone has more unsold homes?

East, in units: 22,878 against 15,408. But North's stock grew faster, up 34% in a year. At the current pace, East would take about 15 months to sell what's built or launched, and North about 12. Both give you room to negotiate on slower projects.

## Where are the jobs?

East, by a wide margin. PBD East took 29.3% of Bengaluru's office leasing in H1 2026, and with the Outer Ring Road, over 81% of the city's leasing. PBD North took 3.2%, and its office rents were flat over 12 months while PBD East's rose 10% (Knight Frank). North's job story is the airport belt, the KIADB Aerospace Park and Manyata Tech Park.

## Which has better metro access?

East. The Purple Line runs to Whitefield. In Knight Frank's North locations, nothing runs yet. The Blue Line will link both zones to the airport. Knight Frank cites a June 2027 target, while more recent news reports put full opening at March 2028, in phases ([Times of India, 9 Oct 2026](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/bengaluru/bengaluru-metro-blue-line-delayed-to-march-2028-how-the-58km-airport-corridor-will-open-in-phases-which-stretches-will-be-ready-first-and-when-commuters-can-expect-services-from-central-silk-board-to-kia/photostory/134829302.cms), news). ## Frequently asked questions

### Is North or East Bengaluru better for buying a home?

It depends on where you work. East has the jobs and a running metro. North is selling faster and has a planned airport-side metro. Compare specific locations, not zones.

### Which zone's prices are rising faster?

Over 12 months, Knight Frank's East locations rose 14–20% and its North locations 6–18%. Over six months, three of the four North locations rose faster.

### Which zone has more unsold homes?

East: 22,878 homes and 4.9 quarters to sell. North had 15,408 and 3.9 quarters, but its stock rose 34% in a year (Knight Frank H1 2026).

### Is East Bengaluru overpriced?

We can't say that from the data. East's prices rose fastest while its sales fell, which can mean more room to negotiate. Compare the price with recent registrations nearby.

### When will North Bengaluru get a metro?

The Blue Line will run through Hebbal to the airport. Knight Frank cites June 2027; news reports put full opening at March 2028, in phases.

### Which zone gives better rental yield?

On ANAROCK's figures, they're level: about 4.2% gross for 1,000 sq ft in both Whitefield and Thanisandra Main Road.

### What counts as North and East Bengaluru?

Sources differ. In Knight Frank's zones, North covers Hebbal, Bellary Road, Hennur, Jakkur, Yelahanka and Banaswadi; East covers Whitefield, Old Airport Road, Old Madras Road, KR Puram and Marathahalli.

### How much are stamp charges?

Inside GBA, charges total 7.6% of the higher of price and guidance value; outside, 7.65%. Try our [stamp duty calculator](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/stamp-duty-and-registration-calculator-karnataka/).
