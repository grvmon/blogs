# North Bengaluru property guide 2026: Hebbal to Devanahalli

**Short answer:** This North Bengaluru property guide compares five areas. Flat prices run from about ₹5,700 to ₹15,000 per sq ft (Knight Frank, H1 2026), with Yelahanka up 18% in a year. No metro runs in this corridor yet; the airport stretch is targeted for September 2027 (news). Guidance values vary by street, and Devanahalli has the highest flood hazard.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **North is growing:** Knight Frank says North Bengaluru launches rose 10% and sales 11% in H1 2026.
> - **One year, very different moves:** Yelahanka +18%, Hebbal +11%, Hennur +10%, Thanisandra +6%.
> - **Supply:** 704 of the 2,692 projects K-RERA approved from 2020 to 15 Oct 2026 are in the North, Yelahanka and Devanahalli taluks (our count).
> - **Flood risk:** an IISc-led study rated over half of Devanahalli taluk as high or very high flood hazard (news).
> - **Our NORTH check:** network, official floor, risk, taluk supply, housing cost.

North Bengaluru stretches from the Hebbal flyover to the airport and beyond. The pitch is jobs near the airport and a metro on the way. This guide pulls together what we've verified area by area, so you can see the corridor at once and then read the area guide that fits.

## Where is new supply going?

Our count of K-RERA approvals from 2020 to 15 Oct 2026 ([taluk study](https://acrenkey.com/insights/new-rera-registered-projects-bengaluru-by-taluk-2020-2026/)):

| Taluk | Projects approved | Share of 2,692 |
|---|---|---|
| Bengaluru North | 329 | 12.2% |
| Devanahalli | 242 | 9.0% |
| Yelahanka | 133 | 4.9% |
| **North belt total** | **704** | **26.2%** |

For comparison, Bengaluru East had 643 and Anekal 603. Devanahalli's 242 projects sit in the taluk with the highest flood hazard share in the IISc-led study, as reported by [Bangalore Mirror](https://bangaloremirror.indiatimes.com/bangalore/civic/red-alert/articleshow/134923907.cms) (news). See our [flood exposure index](https://acrenkey.com/insights/bengaluru-flood-exposure-index-rainfall-vs-rera-project-locations/).

## Worked examples

### Worked example 1: one year of price change on ₹1 crore

Illustrative. A flat worth ₹1,00,00,000 a year ago, moved by each area's Knight Frank 12-month change.

| Area | Change | Value now | Gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yelahanka | +18% | ₹1,18,00,000 | ₹18,00,000 |
| Hebbal | +11% | ₹1,11,00,000 | ₹11,00,000 |
| Hennur | +10% | ₹1,10,00,000 | ₹10,00,000 |
| Thanisandra | +6% | ₹1,06,00,000 | ₹6,00,000 |

A ₹12 lakh gap in one year between two neighbouring areas. One year isn't a trend, and a fast rise can mean less room to rise next. Use it to ask why, not to predict.

### Worked example 2: the street decides the floor

A 1,200 sq ft site is 111.48 sq m. Here's its guidance value, and stamp duty and registration at 7.6% on that floor, at each end of two areas' ranges.

| Area and line | ₹/sq m | Guidance value | Charges on the floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yelahanka, lowest line | 29,100 | ₹32,44,068 | ₹2,46,549 |
| Yelahanka, highest line | 74,410 | ₹82,95,227 | ₹6,30,437 |
| Hennur, lowest line | 25,000 | ₹27,87,000 | ₹2,11,812 |
| Hennur, highest line | 80,000 | ₹89,18,400 | ₹6,77,798 |

Within one area, the floor can vary about threefold. Duty is on the higher of your price and the guidance value, so look up your exact line on Kaveri.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does this North Bengaluru property guide cover?

Hebbal, Yelahanka, Thanisandra, Hennur and Devanahalli: prices, guidance values, metro timing, new RERA supply and flood risk, with links to each area guide.

### What are flat prices in North Bengaluru in 2026?

Knight Frank's H1 2026 ranges run from ₹5,700 per sq ft at the low end of Thanisandra to ₹15,000 at the top of Hebbal. It doesn't publish Devanahalli prices.

### Which North Bengaluru area rose fastest?

Yelahanka, up 18% in the 12 months to H1 2026 on Knight Frank's figures. Thanisandra rose 6%.

### When will the airport metro open?

The Hebbal–airport stretch is targeted for September 2027, according to news reports. The full Blue Line has been pushed to March 2028. Dates have slipped before.

### Does the airport metro reach Devanahalli town?

No. It serves the airport. Devanahalli town is a separate municipal area with its own planning authority and water constraints.

### Is North Bengaluru prone to flooding?

It varies. An IISc-led study rated over half of Devanahalli taluk as high or very high flood hazard, and Yelahanka saw lake breaches in October 2024. Check each site.

### How much new supply is coming in North Bengaluru?

Our count finds 704 K-RERA approvals across the North, Yelahanka and Devanahalli taluks from 2020 to 15 Oct 2026, about a quarter of the city's total.

### Should I choose North or East Bengaluru?

It depends on your commute, budget and risk tolerance. Our North vs East comparison sets them side by side.
