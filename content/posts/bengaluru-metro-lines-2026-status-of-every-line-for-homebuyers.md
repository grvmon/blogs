# Bengaluru metro new lines 2026: the status of every line for homebuyers

**Short answer:** Of the Bengaluru metro new lines in 2026, only the Yellow Line (opened August 2025) is running, alongside the Purple and Green Lines, for about 96 km in all. The Pink Line's 7.5 km elevated stretch awaits safety clearance. The Blue Line to the airport is 72.5% built. The Orange and Red Lines remain years away.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **Running network:** Purple, Green and Yellow Lines operate across approximately 96.10 km.
> - **Next to open:** Pink Line elevated stretch (Kalena Agrahara to Tavarekere, 7.5 km), pending CMRS safety compliance sign-off.
> - **Major build under way:** Blue Line (58.19 km from Central Silk Board to KIA Airport), recording 72.50% physical progress as of mid-2026.
> - **Future phases:** Orange Line (Phase 3, 44.65 km) is in the general consultancy tendering stage; the Red Line (Phase 3A) awaits central cabinet approval.
> - **Framework for buyers:** Use our 5-rung metro ladder to evaluate a station's actual delivery risk before paying a location markup.

If you are buying a home on the strength of a metro line, the line's stage matters far more than its route colour. A running station and an announced one look identical on a marketing brochure map, but they carry vastly different financial values and delivery risks. This guide breaks down the factual status of every corridor as of October 2026.

## Which Bengaluru metro lines are running in 2026?

Three lines are in commercial passenger operation today:

| Line | Route | Length and stations | Running since |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purple | Whitefield (Kadugodi) to Challaghatta | 43.49 km, 37 stations | Full line 9 Oct 2023 |
| Green | Madavara to Silk Institute | 33.50 km, 31 stations | Madavara extension 7 Nov 2024 |
| Yellow | RV Road (Ragigudda) to Bommasandra | Over 19 km, 16 stations | 10 Aug 2025 |

The Yellow Line opened on 10 Aug 2025 ([PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2154848)), connecting South Bengaluru and Electronic City. By August 2026, induction of its 15th train reduced peak-hour headway to 6 minutes ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/bengaluru-namma-metro-inducts-15th-yellow-line-train-peak-hour-services-down-to-six-minutes/article71349951.ece)). Together with the Purple and Green Lines, Bengaluru's active operational metro network spans approximately 96.10 km ([The Hindu, 13 Oct 2026](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/bangalore/bmrcl-bengaluru-namma-metro-pink-line-to-respond-to-cmrs-observations-next-week/article71578875.ece)).

## Which metro lines are under construction?

| Line and stretch | Length | Current status (Oct 2026) | Target completion & benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pink, elevated: Kalena Agrahara–Tavarekere | 7.5 km, 6 stations | CMRS inspected 6–8 Oct and listed 36 observations; BMRCL filed compliance 15 Oct | No official date announced; regional reports indicate potential late-2026 commissioning |
| Pink, underground: Dairy Circle–Nagawara | about 13.8 km | Tunneling and track-laying active; trial runs targeted before end-2026 | Mid-2027 (targeted by BMRCL management) |
| Blue, Silk Board–KR Puram (Outer Ring Road) | Part of 58.19 km | Viaduct civil works advancing; trial runs targeted from late 2026 | Mid-2027 (targeted by BMRCL) |
| Blue, Hebbal–airport | Part of 58.19 km | Pier erection and viaduct work ongoing; stretch beyond Yelahanka pacing behind | September 2027 (ministerial target) |
| Blue, KR Puram–Hebbal | Part of 58.19 km | Civil viaduct work under construction | December 2027 to March 2028 (ministerial target) |

The official progress benchmark for the entire 58.19 km Blue Line stood at 72.50% physical and 70.17% financial progress as of 30 June 2026, per the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs' Lok Sabha disclosure ([Lok Sabha Q.3087](https://sansad.in/getFile/lsapps/loksabhaquestions/annex/188/AU3087_a08f2L.pdf)). That parliamentary response noted that metro projects generally take five to six years from sanction, depending on land acquisition, tree-cutting permissions, and utility clearance.

Earlier Pink Line elevated targets of mid-August and mid-October 2026 were deferred. As a buyer, treat every completion date in this table as an operational target rather than a fixed possession date.

## How should homebuyers read a metro line's stage?

### The 5-rung metro ladder (our framework)

| Rung | What has happened | How much pricing weight to give it |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Proposed | A project report or alignment study exists | **Zero price markup.** Routes frequently shift, stall, or undergo redesigns |
| 2. Approved | Sanctioned by State and Centre with budget allocated | **Low.** Ground construction has not commenced; land acquisition remains |
| 3. Under construction | Civil work active on the ground with quarterly progress filings | **Moderate.** Verify the specific progress on your immediate station stretch |
| 4. Safety cleared | The Commissioner of Metro Rail Safety (CMRS) has inspected and cleared passenger service | **High.** Commercial opening is imminent |
| 5. Running | Commercial passenger trains operate daily on schedule | **Full.** Test real walking access, road crossings, and service frequency |

On this ladder, the Red Line sits on Rung 1, the Orange Line on Rung 2, the Blue Line and the Pink underground on Rung 3, and the Pink elevated stretch between Rungs 3 and 4. Only the Purple, Green, and Yellow Lines sit on Rung 5.

## Worked examples

### Worked example 1: the cost of servicing an unearned metro markup

When builders market an upcoming metro corridor, they routinely charge a 10% to 15% location markup over comparable non-metro inventory. 

Illustrative: Suppose a developer adds a ₹12 lakh "metro markup" to a ₹1 crore property based on a line promised in promotional materials:

| Line marketed by seller | Estimated wait from Oct 2026 | Additional interest paid on a ₹12L markup (at 8.5% p.a.) |
|---|---|---|
| Pink underground (target mid-2027) | about 8 months | ~₹68,000 |
| Blue Line, KR Puram–Hebbal (target March 2028) | about 1.5 years | ~₹1,53,000 |
| Red Line (targeted ~2033) | about 7 years | ~₹7,14,000 |

### Worked example 2: why walking distance decides real utility

Illustrative, calculated at an average urban walking speed of 5 km/h:

| Distance to station | One-way walking time | Extra monthly walking time vs an 800 m station (22 workdays, return) |
|---|---|---|
| 800 m | ~9.6 minutes | Baseline |
| 2.0 km | ~24.0 minutes | ~10.6 extra hours per month |

A station 2 km away adds over 10 hours of monthly commuting on foot. At that distance, daily commuters generally resort to feeder autos or two-wheelers, introducing parking friction and extra costs. ## Metro checklist before you buy

- Verify the corridor's position on the 5-rung ladder before accepting any price markup.
- Walk the actual pedestrian route from the project gate to the nearest station entrance, checking for continuous footpaths, street lighting, and safe road crossings.
- Check the exact status of your immediate station stretch, not just the overall corridor headline.
- Treat every announced opening date as a soft operational target, and ensure your daily commute works without it.
- Compare unit pricing against equivalent developments situated 3 to 5 km outside the metro catchment.
- Inspect whether your apartment faces the elevated viaduct directly to evaluate construction noise and visual privacy.
- Verify any "metro-facing" claims in builder brochures directly against BMRCL's notified alignment maps.
- Have your property lawyer conduct formal title diligence and approval verification as standard practice ([our legal checks guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-property-legal-checks-from-title-to-oc/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which metro lines are running in Bengaluru in 2026?

Three: the Purple Line (Whitefield to Challaghatta), the Green Line (Madavara to Silk Institute) and the Yellow Line (RV Road to Bommasandra, opened August 2025). Together they cover about 96.10 km as of October 2026. The Pink Line's elevated stretch is next in line.

### When will the Pink Line open?

There is no official date announced yet. The 7.5 km elevated stretch from Kalena Agrahara to Tavarekere completed safety inspection on 6–8 Oct 2026, and BMRCL submitted compliance responses on 15 Oct. Commercial opening is targeted for late 2026. The underground stretch to Nagawara is targeted for mid-2027.

### When will the airport metro open?

The Blue Line's Hebbal–airport stretch is targeted for September 2027 and the full 58.19 km line for March 2028, per ministerial announcements in October 2026. Officially, the line recorded 72.50% physical progress as of 30 June 2026, and the central government has not notified a formal completion date.

### What is Namma Metro Phase 3?

Phase 3, the Orange Line, consists of two elevated corridors approved in August 2024: JP Nagar 4th Phase to Kempapura along the western outer ring road (32.15 km), and Hosahalli to Kadabagere along Magadi Road (12.50 km). The approval set a 2029 target; BMRCL invited consultancy bids in October 2026.

### What is the Red Line?

It is the proposed Sarjapura–Hebbal line, covering 37.804 km with 27 stations, as outlined in BMRCL's February 2026 Detailed Project Report. It awaits central cabinet approval. Preliminary soil testing has begun on Sarjapur Road, with commissioning targeted around 2033.

### Should I pay more for a home near an upcoming metro station?

Only for verified, near-term progress. Evaluate the corridor's position on our 5-rung ladder, inspect the physical work on your specific station stretch, and test the real walking route. An unapproved line years away should command zero price markup.

### How far from a metro station is close enough?

At a standard walking pace of 5 km/h, 800 metres takes under 10 minutes, whereas 2 km takes 24 minutes—adding approximately 10 extra hours per month for a daily commuter. Time the physical route on foot, factoring in intersections, road crossings, and footpath continuity.

### Where can I check official metro progress?

BMRCL publishes formal notifications for line openings, and passenger operations require formal clearance from the Commissioner of Metro Rail Safety (CMRS). For construction progress, parliamentary disclosures provide official benchmarks, such as the Blue Line's 72.50% physical progress reported in August 2026.
