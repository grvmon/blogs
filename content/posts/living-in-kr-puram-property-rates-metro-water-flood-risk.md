# KR Puram property rates (2026): metro, water and flood risk

**Short answer:** KR Puram property rates for flats run ₹6,400 to ₹13,400 per sq ft (Knight Frank, H1 2026), up 20% in a year, the fastest rise of the 17 Bengaluru locations it tracks. Official site guidance values run ₹40,000 to ₹75,000 per sq m. The Purple Line already stops here; the Blue Line is due from 2027.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **Fastest riser:** +20% in 12 months, first of Knight Frank's 17 Bengaluru locations.
> - **Metro now:** KR Puram has been on the Purple Line since March 2023. It's set to become the Blue Line's interchange, with the Silk Board stretch reportedly targeted for May 2027 (news).
> - **Floors vary by road:** the Hoodi–KR Puram Main Road site line is nearly twice the Kodigehalli line.
> - **Delivery record:** in Bengaluru East taluk, 63.1% of 1,235 RERA projects still carried their original completion date.
> - **Our 4C check:** corridor, connection, catchment, completion.

KR Puram (Krishnarajapura) sits at the eastern end of the old city, where the outer ring road meets Old Madras Road. Knight Frank counts it in its East zone, with Whitefield, Old Madras Road and Marathahalli. This guide uses official records and named sources; where something isn't known, we say so.

![Map of KR Puram showing the Purple Line station, the planned Blue Line along the outer ring road, Old Madras Road and the Hoodi–KR Puram Main Road](/img/blog/kr-puram-map.png)

## Who governs KR Puram?

KR Puram is inside the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) area, so records are e-Khata on e-Aasthi and the stamp duty surcharge is the city rate of 2% of duty. The gazette still lists it by the old Ward No. 52, but GBA's five city corporations now have 369 wards (notified November 2025). Use our [ward and corporation lookup](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/which-ward-and-city-corporation-is-my-flat-in-gba-pin-code-lookup/) and confirm from the tax receipt. Our [GBA explainer](https://acrenkey.com/insights/gba-replaces-bbmp-what-new-city-corporations-mean-for-buyers/) covers what changed.

## What are property rates in KR Puram?

### Official guidance values

Residential site rates from the 2023 gazette (in force since 1 Oct 2023), Shivajinagar registration-district book:

| Gazette line (residential site) | Per sq m | Per sq ft |
|---|---|---|
| Hoodi to K R Puram Main Road | ₹75,000 | ₹6,968 |
| Kodigehalli to K R Puram Road | ₹50,000 | ₹4,645 |
| Krishnarajapura (Ward No. 52) | ₹46,000 | ₹4,274 |
| Kodigehalli (village) | ₹40,000 | ₹3,716 |

Flats are notified project by project, so look yours up on Kaveri. Our [rates-by-area table](https://acrenkey.com/insights/bengaluru-property-rates-by-area-guidance-value-table-2026/) has more localities. A revision has been reported in the news, but none had been notified as of 19 Oct 2026.

### Market prices

Knight Frank's H1 2026 report puts KR Puram flat prices at **₹6,400 to ₹13,400 per sq ft**, up **20%** over 12 months. That was the largest rise of the 17 Bengaluru locations in its table, ahead of Yelahanka (18%) and Tumkur Road (16%).

The two big consultants measure the East differently. ANAROCK says the East took 47% of Bengaluru's housing absorption in Q1 2026. Knight Frank says the East had 29% of H1 2026 sales, with sales down 9% year on year. Different measures and periods, so neither is wrong.

We didn't find a published KR Puram rent figure from a named consultant, so we don't quote one. Ask local agents for signed leases in the building you're considering.

### What's being built, and does it finish on time?

KR Puram falls in Bengaluru East taluk on Karnataka RERA. In our [East RERA study](https://acrenkey.com/insights/do-east-bengaluru-projects-deliver-on-their-rera-date/), the taluk had 1,235 approved housing and plotted projects (and compare commute corridors against ITPL in our [KR Puram vs Whitefield guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/kr-puram-vs-whitefield-for-homebuyers/); for lakeside project diligence, see our review of [Brigade Lakecrest](https://acrenkey.com/property/brigade-lakecrest-kr-puram/)), and 63.1% still carried their original completion date. The taluk also covers Whitefield and Mahadevapura, so these aren't KR Puram-only figures.

## Is there a metro in KR Puram?

Yes.

| Line | What it does | Status (Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Purple Line | KR Puram to Whitefield (Kadugodi) east, and across the city to Challaghatta | Running. The Whitefield–KR Puram stretch (13.71 km, 12 stations) opened in March 2023; the full 43.49 km, 37-station line since 9 Oct 2023 |
| Blue Line, Silk Board–KR Puram | Along the outer ring road to the south-east IT belt | BMRCL reported to be aiming for May 2027 (news) |
| Blue Line, KR Puram–Hebbal | North along the ring road towards Hebbal and the airport line | Expected December 2027 to March 2028 (news) |

Sources: The Hindu (October 2023, quoting BMRCL), BLR Today (17 Oct 2026) and Times of India (9 Oct 2026), all news; station and terminus names are per those reports. A station in the area doesn't mean one near your gate, so walk the route.

## Where does KR Puram get its water?

Older parts of KR Puram were in the city before 2007. The added villages around it fall in Cauvery Stage 5's Mahadevapura zone (JICA). By May 2026, about 1.15 lakh of an estimated 1.8 lakh buildings in Stage 5 villages were connected ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/bengalurus-water-woes-piped-connections-sanchari-cauvery-reduce-tanker-demand/article70978412.ece), news). So check each building. Our [Cauvery Stage 5 guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/cauvery-stage-5-coverage-which-areas-get-bwssb-water/) shows how.

## Is KR Puram flood-prone?

Some streets are. In April 2026, the East corporation had 20 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations, and 52 of the 101 citywide were still unfixed ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/bengaluru/2026/Apr/11/52-of-101-flood-prone-locations-in-bengaluru-yet-to-be-fixed-before-monsoon), news). We don't have a street list for KR Puram. The state's [Varunamitra vulnerable spots map](https://varunamitra.karnataka.gov.in/Default/Index?service=VulnerableSpots) is the official place to look.

Lake buffers became size-based (0 to 30 m) under Act 19 of 2026, and primary drains carry a 50 m buffer under RMP-2015. Our [lake and drain checks](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/flood-risk-lake-buffers-and-rajakaluve-checks-before-you-buy/) explain the steps.

## Worked examples

### Worked example 1: duty on a KR Puram flat

Illustrative. A 1,100 sq ft flat at ₹9,900 per sq ft (the midpoint of Knight Frank's range) = ₹1,08,90,000.

| Charge | Working | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Stamp duty 5% | 5% × ₹1,08,90,000 | ₹5,44,500 |
| Cess 10% of duty | 10% × ₹5,44,500 | ₹54,450 |
| Surcharge 2% of duty | 2% × ₹5,44,500 | ₹10,890 |
| Registration 2% | 2% × ₹1,08,90,000 | ₹2,17,800 |
| **Total (7.6%)** | | **₹8,27,640** |

With 20% down, the ₹87,12,000 loan over 20 years at an assumed 8.5% is an EMI of about ₹75,605. Try your own numbers on the [EMI calculator](https://acrenkey.com/calculators/home-loan-emi-calculator/), and see the [full cost guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/full-cost-bengaluru-flat-stamp-duty-registration-gst/) for GST and other costs.

### Worked example 2: what a 20% rise does to your costs

Illustrative. The same 1,100 sq ft flat at ₹9,000 per sq ft a year ago, up 20%.

| Item | A year ago | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Price per sq ft | ₹9,000 | ₹10,800 |
| Flat price | ₹99,00,000 | ₹1,18,80,000 |
| Charges at 7.6% | ₹7,52,400 | ₹9,02,880 |

The price is up ₹19,80,000, and charges are up ₹1,50,480 on top. A local average rise doesn't tell you what your building is worth, so check its past registered values on encumbrance certificates.

### Worked example 3: a 1,200 sq ft site on three lines

Illustrative.

| Gazette line | Guidance value | Minimum charges at 7.6% |
|---|---|---|
| Kodigehalli, ₹40,000 per sq m | ₹44,59,350 | ₹3,38,911 |
| Krishnarajapura, ₹46,000 per sq m | ₹51,28,253 | ₹3,89,747 |
| Hoodi–K R Puram Main Road, ₹75,000 per sq m | ₹83,61,282 | ₹6,35,457 |

The main road floor is 1.875 times the Kodigehalli line. Confirm the line for your survey number before you agree a price.

## What mistakes do buyers make in KR Puram?

- **Treating +20% as a forecast.** It's last year's change for new stock.
- **Counting the Blue Line as open.** Only the Purple Line runs today.
- **Pricing a side street off the main road line.**
- **Assuming the old city part has no water gaps.** Check the building.

*This is general information, not legal or tax advice. Ask a property lawyer to check title and approvals, and a CA about the tax side.*

## Frequently asked questions

### What are property rates in KR Puram in 2026?

Knight Frank's H1 2026 report puts KR Puram flat prices at ₹6,400 to ₹13,400 per sq ft, up 20% in a year, the fastest rise of its 17 Bengaluru locations. Official site guidance values run from ₹40,000 per sq m in Kodigehalli to ₹75,000 on the Hoodi–KR Puram Main Road.

### Is KR Puram good to live in?

It suits people who want a running metro and quick access to the eastern IT belt. Against that, traffic at the junctions is heavy, water varies by building, and some East streets flood. Run the corridor, connection, catchment, completion check on each property.

### Is there a metro in KR Puram?

Yes. KR Puram has been on the Purple Line since March 2023, with trains to Whitefield and across the city. The Blue Line along the outer ring road is due next: Silk Board–KR Puram reportedly targeted for May 2027, and KR Puram–Hebbal expected December 2027 to March 2028.

### Does KR Puram get Cauvery water?

Many buildings do. The added villages around KR Puram are in Cauvery Stage 5's Mahadevapura zone, where about 1.15 lakh of 1.8 lakh buildings were connected by May 2026. Ask for the building's BWSSB connection number and recent bills before you pay anything.

### Is KR Puram flood-prone?

Some streets are. In April 2026, 20 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations were in the East corporation, and 52 citywide were still unfixed. These are points, not whole areas, so check the nearest lake and drain, look at the Varunamitra map and visit after heavy rain.

### What is the guidance value in KR Puram?

Under the 2023 gazette, Krishnarajapura (Ward No. 52) is ₹46,000 per sq m for sites, Kodigehalli ₹40,000, Kodigehalli to K R Puram Road ₹50,000 and Hoodi to K R Puram Main Road ₹75,000. Flats are notified project by project on Kaveri.

### Do KR Puram projects finish on time?

We can't say for KR Puram alone. In Bengaluru East taluk, which includes it, 63.1% of 1,235 RERA projects still carried their original completion date in our study. Check each project's RERA page for extensions before you book.

### How much stamp duty will I pay in KR Puram?

About 7.6% of the price: 5% duty, 10% cess and 2% surcharge on the duty, plus 2% registration. On a ₹1.089 crore flat that's ₹8,27,640. If the guidance value is higher than the price, charges go on the guidance value.
