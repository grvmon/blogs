# Kanakapura Road vs Bannerghatta Road: what the data says

**Short answer:** Kanakapura Road vs Bannerghatta Road comes down to price and metro timing. Knight Frank puts Kanakapura Road at ₹5,300–10,100 per sq ft and Bannerghatta Road at ₹7,600–15,500. Kanakapura Road's Green Line stations have run since 2021. Bannerghatta Road's Pink Line has been built and inspected but has no opening date yet.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **Price gap:** at the midpoints, a 1,000 sq ft flat costs ₹38.5 lakh more on Bannerghatta Road (Knight Frank H1 2026).
> - **Growth:** +6% in 12 months on Kanakapura Road, +7% on Bannerghatta Road, with +15% in six months on Bannerghatta Road.
> - **Metro:** five Green Line stations running on Kanakapura Road; six Pink Line stations on Bannerghatta Road awaiting authorisation (news).
> - **Shared link:** the Kothanur–Gottigere Main Road joins the two, with one guidance value of ₹70,850 per sq m.
> - **Our MILE test:** metro status, interior or main road, lake and low ground, entry gap.

Both roads run south from the city a few kilometres apart, and both are in the South City Corporation on their inner stretches. This page puts our [Kanakapura Road guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/living-on-kanakapura-road-property-rates-metro-water-flood-risk/) and [Bannerghatta Road guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/living-on-bannerghatta-road-property-rates-metro-water-flood-risk/) side by side.

## Which is cheaper, Kanakapura Road or Bannerghatta Road?

Kanakapura Road, by a wide margin at the top end. Its range tops out at ₹10,100 per sq ft, while Bannerghatta Road's reaches ₹15,500. The low ends are closer: ₹5,300 against ₹7,600.

Official floors tell a similar story near the city. Bannerghatta Road's inner main-road properties are valued at up to ₹1,98,900 per sq m, more than double Kanakapura Road's highest line. Further out, the gap narrows: Gottigere sits at ₹47,150 and Thalaghattapura at ₹42,000.

## Which has better metro access?

Today, Kanakapura Road. The Green Line runs through Konanakunte Cross, Doddakallasandra, Vajarahalli, Thalaghattapura and Silk Institute, opened in January 2021 ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=1688594)).

Bannerghatta Road's elevated Pink Line, Kalena Agrahara to Tavarekere, is built. The safety commissioner inspected it on 6–8 October 2026 and raised 36 observations ([News18](https://www.news18.com/cities/bengaluru-news/waiting-for-bengalurus-pink-line-not-yet-36-safety-fixes-still-pending-before-it-opens-10382674.html), news). The state has asked the Prime Minister for an inauguration date ([Deccan Herald, 12 Oct 2026](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/cm-shivakumar-seeks-date-from-pm-to-open-elevated-pink-line-cmrs-flags-observations-4180347), news). No opening date had been announced by 19 October. Station names per news reports. Our [metro lines guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-metro-lines-2026-status-of-every-line-for-homebuyers/) tracks it.

## Which has better water supply?

On the inner stretches, they're alike: older layouts get BWSSB Cauvery water, and the villages added in 2007 are being connected through Cauvery Stage 5. By May 2026, about 1.15 lakh of an estimated 1.8 lakh buildings in those villages were connected ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/bengalurus-water-woes-piped-connections-sanchari-cauvery-reduce-tanker-demand/article70978412.ece), news). Outside GBA, BWSSB doesn't supply. See our [Cauvery Stage 5 guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/cauvery-stage-5-coverage-which-areas-get-bwssb-water/).

## Which is more flood-prone?

Bannerghatta Road has the bigger recorded event. The Hulimavu lake bund breached on 24 November 2019 and flooded more than 800 houses ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/bangalore/clean-up-efforts-begin-after-hulimavu-lake-breach/article30074583.ece), news). For both, the South City Corporation had 24 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations in April 2026 ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/bengaluru/2026/Apr/11/52-of-101-flood-prone-locations-in-bengaluru-yet-to-be-fixed-before-monsoon), news). Use our [lake and drain checks](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/flood-risk-lake-buffers-and-rajakaluve-checks-before-you-buy/) for the specific site.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Kanakapura Road cheaper than Bannerghatta Road?

Yes, on Knight Frank's H1 2026 ranges: ₹5,300–10,100 per sq ft against ₹7,600–15,500. At the midpoints, a 1,000 sq ft flat is ₹38.5 lakh cheaper on Kanakapura Road.

### Which road has a working metro?

Kanakapura Road. Five Green Line stations have run since January 2021. Bannerghatta Road's Pink Line is built but had no opening date as of 19 October 2026 (news).

### Which has grown faster?

Over 12 months, they're close: +6% on Kanakapura Road and +7% on Bannerghatta Road. Knight Frank shows +15% over six months on Bannerghatta Road.

### Will the Pink Line raise Bannerghatta Road prices?

Nobody can say how much. Some prices may already reflect it. Base your budget on today's figures, not a forecast.

### Which is more flood-prone?

Bannerghatta Road has the bigger record: the 2019 Hulimavu lake breach flooded more than 800 houses. On both roads, check the specific site against lakes and drains.

### Do both get Cauvery water?

The inner areas do, through BWSSB. Villages added in 2007 are being connected through Stage 5. Outside GBA, expect borewells and tankers.

### Is there a road between the two?

Yes. The Kothanur–Gottigere Main Road links them, with a guidance value of ₹70,850 per sq m for properties along it.

### How much are stamp charges on each road?

Inside GBA, charges total 7.6% of the higher of price and guidance value. On a ₹77 lakh flat that's ₹5,85,200. Use our [stamp duty calculator](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/stamp-duty-and-registration-calculator-karnataka/).
