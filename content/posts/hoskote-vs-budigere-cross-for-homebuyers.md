# Hoskote vs Budigere Cross: the data side by side

**Short answer:** Hoskote vs Budigere Cross is a comparison with thin official data on both sides. Neither has a Knight Frank or ANAROCK price. Guidance values are close: ₹26,000 per sq m on Hoskote's Old Madras Road line and ₹30,310 in Avalahalli near Budigere Cross. Both are stops on a proposed KR Puram–Hoskote metro.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **No consultant price for either:** ANAROCK names Budigere Cross as an East micro-market but publishes no rate; portals show asking prices, not registrations.
> - **Guidance values:** Hoskote town lines run ₹18,000 to ₹30,000 per sq m; villages around Budigere Cross ₹22,000 to ₹32,500.
> - **Metro is a plan:** BMRCL is preparing a project report for a 16.3 km KR Puram–Hoskote line with stations at Avalahalli, Budigere Cross and Hoskote (news).
> - **Outside the city:** both sit mostly outside GBA, so stamp charges are 7.65% and water depends on borewells and tankers unless a project proves otherwise.
> - **Our EDGE test:** entity, drainage, guidance gap, expected transit.

Both sit on Old Madras Road (NH-75) east of Whitefield. Budigere Cross is the junction about halfway to Hoskote, surrounded by panchayat villages in Bengaluru East taluk; Hoskote is a town with its own municipal council. This comparison reuses our [Hoskote guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/living-in-hoskote-property-rates-metro-water-flood-risk/) and adds gazette data for the villages around Budigere Cross. For project reviews along Budigere road, see [Brigade Calista](https://acrenkey.com/property/brigade-calista-budigere-cross/) and [Sattva Songbird](https://acrenkey.com/property/sattva-songbird-budigere-road/).

Guidance values are the 2023 rates still in force. Check Kaveri for your registration date; our [guidance value table](https://acrenkey.com/insights/bengaluru-property-rates-by-area-guidance-value-table-2026/) explains why.

## What are property rates in Hoskote and Budigere Cross?

Only official floors are available for both. Hoskote town's lines run from ₹18,000 per sq m on Madras Road East to ₹26,000 on Old Madras Road, with some private layouts at up to ₹30,000.

We couldn't find a gazette row named Budigere Cross. The villages around the junction, in the KR Puram sub-registrar area, are valued at:

| Gazette line (residential site) | Guidance value (₹/sq m) | Per sq ft (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Avalahalli, main road attached | 32,500 | 3,019 |
| Avalahalli | 30,310 | 2,816 |
| Bidarahalli, main road attached | 25,000 | 2,323 |
| Bidarahalli | 22,000 | 2,044 |
| Mandur | 22,000 | 2,044 |

Portals publish asking prices for both, often well above these floors. Those aren't registered values, so pull encumbrance certificates for recent sales near the property.

## Which has better metro prospects?

They share one plan. BMRCL is preparing a project report for a 16.3 km line from KR Puram to Hoskote with 11 stations, including Avalahalli, Budigere Cross, Hoskote Toll Plaza and Hoskote Government Hospital ([Construction World, 8 Oct 2026](https://www.constructionworld.in/transport-infrastructure/metro-rail-and-railways-infrastructure/hoskote-metro-link-plans-11-stations-from-kr-puram/98379), news). Station names per news reports. It still needs technical, financial and land approvals, and has no date.

Budigere Cross is closer to the running Purple Line at Whitefield (Kadugodi). Our [metro lines guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-metro-lines-2026-status-of-every-line-for-homebuyers/) tracks the rest.

## Who runs each area, and why does it matter?

Hoskote town has a City Municipal Council, so records sit on e-Aasthi. Budigere Cross is mostly panchayat land, with records on e-Swathu and layout approvals from BDA or BMRDA depending on the location. Addresses outside GBA pay 7.65% in total stamp charges, against 7.6% inside the city. Our [approvals guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bda-bmrda-gba-or-panchayat-checking-approvals-before-you-buy/) shows which authority to check.

## Which has better water supply?

Neither is on Cauvery Stage 5, which serves the 110 villages inside the former BBMP limits. A state pre-feasibility study found Hoskote had no surface source and relied on borewells, and AMRUT 2.0 water works were tendered in October 2024. We found no public scheme for the villages around Budigere Cross. Ask every project for its water source in writing; our [water questions guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/water-bengaluru-bwssb-borewell-tanker-questions-to-ask/) lists what to ask.

## Which floods more?

An IISc-led study found nearly 40% of Hoskote taluk's area faces high to very high flood hazard ([city news report, 14 Oct 2026](https://bangaloremirror.indiatimes.com/bangalore/civic/red-alert/articleshow/134923907.cms), news). We didn't find a comparable figure for Bengaluru East taluk, where Budigere Cross sits. That's taluk-wide, not plot-level. Check lakes, drains and low ground with our [lake and drain checks](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/flood-risk-lake-buffers-and-rajakaluve-checks-before-you-buy/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Hoskote or Budigere Cross cheaper?

On guidance values, they're close: ₹18,000 to ₹30,000 per sq m in Hoskote town and ₹22,000 to ₹32,500 in villages around Budigere Cross. No consultant publishes a market price for either.

### Does Budigere Cross have a metro?

Not yet. It's a proposed station on a 16.3 km KR Puram–Hoskote line that BMRCL is still planning (news). The nearest running station is Whitefield (Kadugodi) on the Purple Line.

### Is Budigere Cross inside GBA?

Mostly not. Our lookup puts only about 7% of PIN 560049 inside GBA. Most addresses are under gram panchayats, with records on e-Swathu.

### Which has better water?

Neither is on Cauvery Stage 5. Hoskote relies largely on borewells, with AMRUT 2.0 works tendered in 2024. Ask any project near Budigere Cross for its water source in writing.

### Which is more flood-prone?

An IISc-led study rated nearly 40% of Hoskote taluk as high to very high flood hazard. We found no comparable figure for Budigere Cross. Check the specific plot.

### Are there many plotted layouts?

In Hosakote taluk, 112 of 233 RERA projects (48%) are plotted. Budigere Cross projects register under nearby taluks, so we can't isolate them.

### Which is closer to Whitefield?

Budigere Cross, which sits about halfway along Old Madras Road between Whitefield and Hoskote. Time the peak-hour drive from the specific project.

### How much stamp duty will I pay?

Most addresses in both are outside GBA, so charges total 7.65% of the higher of price and guidance value. On a ₹45 lakh plot, that's ₹3,44,250. Try our [stamp duty calculator](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/stamp-duty-and-registration-calculator-karnataka/).
