# Real estate terms India: a Bengaluru homebuyer glossary of 150 terms from A-Khata to UDS

**Short answer:** This glossary explains 150 real estate terms India's homebuyers meet, with a Bengaluru focus. It's grouped by topic: area, documents, approvals and land records, khata and tax, RERA and disputes, money and charges, loans, and the building. Each term gets a plain definition and, where it matters, what to check.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **150 terms in 8 groups**, alphabetical within each group, with jump links below.
> - **Some terms have two meanings.** "CC" can mean commencement or completion certificate. Always ask which.
> - **Area terms move money.** Always price on RERA carpet area.
> - **Karnataka has its own vocabulary:** khata, e-Aasthi, e-Swathu, RTC, Kaveri, guidance value.
> - **Our OMP decode:** ask whether a term changes your Ownership, your Money, or your Permission to build, live or sell.

Property paperwork in India mixes central law, state rules and local jargon. A Bengaluru sale deed might mention UDS, a khata number, a survey number and an EC in one paragraph. This page is a reference: find the term, read the plain meaning, and follow the link if you need the full check.

## How do you decode an unfamiliar term? (the OMP decode)

When you meet a word you don't know in a document, ask what it changes. That tells you who to ask.

| If the term changes your... | Examples | Who to ask |
|---|---|---|
| **Ownership** | Title, sale deed, UDS, JDA share, mortgage, encumbrance | Your lawyer |
| **Money** | Guidance value, GST, stamp duty, loading, LTV, TDS | Your CA or lender, and your own maths |
| **Permission** to build, live or sell | Sanctioned plan, OC, khata, conversion order, setback | The approving authority's records, and your lawyer |

Most terms below are tagged O, M or P so you can see which bucket they fall in.

## What do the area terms mean?

1. **Balcony area (M).** The balcony's floor area. RERA carpet area excludes exclusive balconies and verandas, so check how the agreement lists them.
2. **Built-up area (M).** Carpet area plus the thickness of the flat's walls, and sometimes the balcony. Definitions vary by builder.
3. **Carpet area, RERA (M).** The net usable floor area of a flat, excluding external walls, service shafts and exclusive balconies, but including internal partition walls (RERA Act s.2(k)). It must be stated in the agreement.
4. **Common areas (O).** Lobbies, lifts, staircases, terraces, grounds and amenities shared by all owners. They're handed to the association.
5. **Floor area ratio, FAR or FSI (P).** The total floor area allowed on a plot divided by the plot area. It sets how much can be built.
6. **Loading (M).** The gap between super built-up and carpet area, as a percentage of carpet area. Higher loading means you pay for more space you can't use (use our [carpet area and loading calculator](/blogs/carpet-area-and-loading-calculator-bengaluru/) to verify your numbers).
7. **Plinth area (M).** The covered area at floor level including walls, used in construction estimates.
8. **Plot area (O).** The area of the land parcel, usually in sq ft or sq m; in rural records, in acres and guntas.
9. **Saleable area (M).** A market term, usually meaning super built-up area.
10. **Setback (P).** The open space that must be left between a building and the plot boundary under zoning rules.
11. **Super built-up area (M).** Built-up area plus your share of common areas. Many quotes use it, which hides the loading.
12. **Terrace rights (O).** Whether an owner has exclusive use of a terrace. Check the agreement and sale deed.
13. **Undivided share, UDS (O).** Your proportionate share of the land under the building, registered in your name with the flat.

### Worked example 1: what's your UDS?

A project sits on 1 acre (43,560 sq ft). The total super built-up area of all flats is 60,000 sq ft. Your flat is 1,500 sq ft super built-up.

| Step | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Your share of total area | 1,500 ÷ 60,000 | 2.5% |
| Your UDS | 2.5% × 43,560 sq ft | **1,089 sq ft** |

Builders don't all use super built-up as the basis; some use carpet area. Check the method in the agreement, and check the sale deed states the UDS. A smaller UDS than expected can matter if the building is ever redeveloped.

## What do the document and deed terms mean?

14. **Absolute sale deed (O).** A sale deed that transfers full ownership with no conditions.
15. **Agreement for sale (O).** The contract between buyer and builder before the sale deed. Under RERA, it must be registered before the builder takes more than 10% of the price (s.13).
16. **Allotment letter (O).** The builder's letter confirming which unit is allotted to you.
17. **Booking form (M).** The application you sign when paying the booking amount. Read its cancellation terms.
18. **Conveyance deed (O).** Any deed that transfers title. A sale deed is the commonest kind.
19. **Demand letter (M).** The builder's request for the next instalment. Check it matches the construction stage.
20. **Encumbrance certificate, EC (O).** A record of registered transactions on a property for a chosen period, from Kaveri.
21. **Form 15 and Form 16 (O).** In Karnataka, an EC in Form 15 lists registered transactions; Form 16 says none were found for the period.
22. **General power of attorney, GPA (O).** Authority for someone to act for you in many matters. Sales by GPA alone don't transfer ownership.
23. **Gift deed (O).** A registered deed transferring property without payment.
24. **Legal heir certificate (O).** An official document naming the heirs of a deceased person, often needed for resale of inherited property.
25. **Mother deed (O).** The earliest title document in the chain, showing how the seller's predecessors got the property.
26. **Nomination (O).** Naming who receives your rights in a housing society or bank record. It doesn't replace a will.
27. **No-dues certificate (M).** A letter confirming nothing more is owed, from the builder, society or lender.
28. **Partition deed (O).** A deed dividing jointly held property among co-owners.
29. **Possession letter (O).** The builder's letter handing over the flat. Read it before signing for any waiver.
30. **Power of attorney, special (O).** Authority for one defined act, such as registering one sale.
31. **Rectification deed (O).** A deed correcting an error in an earlier registered deed.
32. **Release or relinquishment deed (O).** A co-owner gives up their share to other co-owners.
33. **Sale deed (O).** The registered document that transfers ownership to you.
34. **Title (O).** The legal right to own a property.
35. **Title search report (O).** A lawyer's report on the title chain and encumbrances.
36. **Will (O).** A document setting out who inherits property after death.

## What do the approval and land record terms mean?

37. **Approved layout (P).** A plotted layout sanctioned by the planning authority. Plots outside one may face loan and khata problems.
38. **BDA (P).** The city's development authority, which plans the core planning area and forms layouts.
39. **Bhoomi (O).** Karnataka's digital land records system for agricultural land (RTCs).
40. **BMRDA (P).** The metropolitan region development authority, covering the wider region around the city and its local planning authorities.
41. **Commencement certificate, CC (P).** Permission to start construction after the plan is sanctioned.
42. **Completion certificate (P).** Confirmation that construction is complete as per the plan. Also abbreviated CC, so ask which one is meant.
43. **Conversion order (P).** The Deputy Commissioner's order allowing agricultural land to be used for non-agricultural purposes, under s.95 of the Karnataka Land Revenue Act.
44. **Deviation (P).** Construction that departs from the sanctioned plan. Beyond permitted limits, it can block the occupancy certificate.
45. **DTCP (P).** The Town and Country Planning department, which approves layouts in some areas outside the major planning authorities.
46. **e-Swathu (O).** Karnataka's online property records for gram panchayat areas.
47. **Encroachment (O).** Building on land you don't own, including government land or drain buffers.
48. **GBA (P).** Greater Bengaluru Authority, which replaced BBMP under the Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, with new city corporations below it.
49. **KIADB (P).** Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Board, which acquires and allots industrial land.
50. **Lake buffer (P).** The no-build zone around a lake, set by planning rules and court orders.
51. **Master plan (P).** The statutory land-use plan for a planning area. Bengaluru's current one is the Revised Master Plan 2015.
52. **NOC (P).** No objection certificate from an authority (fire, airport, pollution) or a lender.
53. **Occupancy certificate, OC (P).** Permission to occupy a building after checking it against the sanctioned plan.
54. **Rajakaluve (P).** A primary storm-water drain. Buffers apply on either side.
55. **Revenue site (P).** A plot on unconverted agricultural land, sold without layout approval.
56. **RTC (O).** Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops (also called pahani), the land record for agricultural land.
57. **Sanctioned plan (P).** The building plan approved by the planning authority.
58. **Survey number (O).** The land parcel's number in revenue records.
59. **TDR (P).** Transferable development rights: extra buildable area given for land surrendered (for example, to road widening), which can be used elsewhere.
60. **TOD zone (P).** Transit-oriented development zones near metro lines, where extra FAR may be allowed.
61. **Zoning regulations (P).** Rules on land use, height, FAR and setbacks within a master plan.

## What do the khata and tax terms mean?

62. **A-Khata (P).** The property register for properties that comply with approvals and pay tax. Banks prefer it.
63. **B-Khata (P).** A separate register for properties with approval or bye-law gaps. It records tax; it doesn't regularise the property.
64. **Capital gains (M).** Profit on selling a property. Tax depends on how long you held it.
65. **CGAS (M).** Capital Gains Accounts Scheme: a bank deposit to park gains you plan to reinvest in a house.
66. **e-Aasthi (P).** The online property record system for Bengaluru's city corporations, which issues e-khatas.
67. **e-Khata (P).** The digital khata record. It's a format, not a third type: an e-khata is either A or B.
68. **Indexation (M).** Adjusting purchase cost for inflation. Now an option only for property bought before 23 July 2024, under conditions. Ask your CA.
69. **Khata certificate (P).** Confirms the property is on the corporation's register.
70. **Khata extract (P).** Gives details of the property as recorded in the register.
71. **Khata transfer (P).** Updating the khata to the new owner after a sale.
72. **Long-term capital gains (M).** Gains on property held more than 24 months, taxed at 12.5% under the current rules.
73. **Mutation (O).** Updating revenue or municipal records after a transfer. It doesn't itself prove title.
74. **Property tax (M).** The annual tax paid to the city corporation or panchayat.
75. **TDS on property (M).** A buyer of property worth ₹50 lakh or more deducts 1% tax from the payment and deposits it.

## What do the RERA and dispute terms mean?

76. **Adjudicating Officer (O).** The RERA officer who decides compensation claims (s.71).
77. **Allottee (O).** The person a flat is allotted, sold or transferred to by the promoter.
78. **Appellate Tribunal (O).** Hears appeals against RERA orders. A promoter must deposit at least 30% of the penalty, or the amount due to the allottee, to appeal (s.43(5)).
79. **Apartment association (O).** The owners' body that takes over common areas. The promoter must enable its formation (s.11(4)(e)).
80. **Consumer commission (O).** District, State or National body hearing consumer complaints, tiered by amount paid.
81. **Defect liability period (M).** Five years from possession, during which the promoter must fix structural and workmanship defects within 30 days of notice (s.14(3)).
82. **Designated account (M).** The separate bank account where a promoter must keep 70% of buyers' money (s.4(2)(l)(D)).
83. **Exempt project (P).** A project on land of 500 sq m or less, or with eight flats or fewer, which needn't register (s.3(2)(a)).
84. **Extension (P).** An extension of a project's RERA completion date. It doesn't change your agreement.
85. **Force majeure (M).** Events outside the promoter's control, like natural disasters, that can extend timelines.
86. **K-RERA (P).** The Karnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority.
87. **Model agreement (O).** The agreement for sale format in the Karnataka RERA Rules.
88. **Promoter (O).** The person or company developing and selling the project.
89. **Quarterly update (P).** The progress report a promoter files on the RERA portal each quarter.
90. **RERA (P).** The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016.
91. **RERA registration number (P).** The project's number on the state RERA portal, which adverts must display (s.11(2)).
92. **Section 18 (M).** The RERA section giving refund or delay interest if possession is late. In Karnataka, interest is SBI's highest MCLR plus 2%.

## What do the money and charge terms mean?

93. **Affordable housing, GST (M).** Flats with carpet area up to 60 sq m in metros and value up to ₹45 lakh, which attract 1% GST instead of 5%.
94. **Booking amount (M).** The first payment to reserve a unit. It counts toward the 10% cap before the agreement is registered.
95. **Cancellation charges (M).** The amount forfeited if you cancel. Check the agreement.
96. **Cess and surcharge (M).** Additions to stamp duty in Karnataka: a 10% cess and 2% surcharge on the duty.
97. **Clubhouse charges (M).** A one-time fee for amenities, often outside the base price.
98. **Construction-linked plan (M).** Payments due at construction stages rather than dates.
99. **Corpus fund (M).** A one-time payment into the association's reserve.
100. **Down payment (M).** The part of the price you pay from your own money, not the loan.
101. **E-stamp (M).** Electronic stamp paper for paying stamp duty.
102. **Escalation clause (M).** A clause letting the builder raise the price for cost increases. Read it closely.
103. **Floor-rise charge (M).** An extra charge per sq ft for higher floors.
104. **GST (M).** 5% on most under-construction flats; none on resale or completed flats with an OC.
105. **Guidance value (M).** The government's minimum value per unit area. Stamp duty is paid on the higher of price and guidance value. The 2023 rates are still in force; check Kaveri.
106. **Kaveri 2.0 (O).** Karnataka's online registration portal, for ECs, guidance values and appointments.
107. **Maintenance charges (M).** Monthly or advance charges for running the building.
108. **Preferred location charge, PLC (M).** An extra charge for a corner, park-facing or other preferred unit.
109. **Registration fee (M).** 2% of the value in Karnataka since 31 August 2025.
110. **Sinking fund (M).** A reserve for major repairs like lift or roof replacement.
111. **Stamp duty (M).** A state tax on the sale deed. With cess, surcharge and registration, about 7.6% in Bengaluru for most flats.
112. **Sub-registrar office (O).** Where documents are registered.
113. **Token amount (M).** An informal deposit to hold a unit. Get the refund terms in writing.

### Worked example 2: stamp duty on the higher value

You agree to buy a resale flat for ₹75 lakh. The guidance value on Kaveri works out to ₹82 lakh.

| Step | Amount |
|---|---|
| Agreed price | ₹75,00,000 |
| Guidance value | ₹82,00,000 |
| Value used (the higher) | ₹82,00,000 |
| Stamp duty, cess, surcharge and registration at 7.6% | **₹6,23,200** |
| If paid on the price instead | ₹5,70,000 |

You pay ₹53,200 more than you'd expect from the price alone. Lower rates apply to some first-sale flats up to ₹45 lakh. See our [full cost guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/full-cost-bengaluru-flat-stamp-duty-registration-gst/).

## What do the home loan terms mean?

114. **Balance transfer (M).** Moving your loan to another lender for a lower rate.
115. **CERSAI (O).** The central registry where lenders record mortgages. A search can reveal an existing charge.
116. **Co-applicant (M).** A second borrower whose income counts toward the loan.
117. **Credit score (M).** A number summarising your repayment history, used to price your loan.
118. **Disbursement (M).** Release of loan money, by stage for under-construction flats.
119. **EMI (M).** Equated monthly instalment. At an illustrative 8.5% over 20 years, about ₹867.82 per lakh.
120. **Fixed rate (M).** A rate that doesn't change for a set period.
121. **Floating rate (M).** A rate linked to a benchmark, which moves with it.
122. **Lien (O).** A right over property until a debt is paid.
123. **Loan-to-value, LTV (M).** The loan as a share of the property's value. RBI caps it at 90%, 80% or 75% by loan size.
124. **MCLR (M).** A bank's marginal cost of funds-based lending rate, an older loan benchmark.
125. **Mortgage (O).** Security over property for a loan. The lender's charge is removed on repayment.
126. **Pre-EMI (M).** Interest-only payments on the amount disbursed, before full EMIs start.
127. **Prepayment (M).** Paying off part or all of the loan early.
128. **Repo rate (M).** RBI's policy rate, which external-benchmark loans follow.
129. **Sanction letter (M).** The lender's approval stating the loan amount and terms.
130. **Subvention scheme (M).** A plan where the builder pays pre-EMI interest for a period. Check what happens if the builder stops.

### Worked example 3: loan and EMI on an ₹80 lakh flat

| Step | Amount |
|---|---|
| Flat value | ₹80,00,000 |
| Maximum loan at 80% LTV (₹30 to ₹75 lakh tier) | ₹64,00,000 |
| Your down payment | ₹16,00,000 |
| EMI at 8.5% over 20 years (64 × ₹867.82) | **₹55,540 a month** |

Add stamp duty and registration (about ₹6.08 lakh at 7.6%), which the bank won't fund for loans above ₹10 lakh under RBI's rules, to your cash need.

## What do the building and services terms mean?

131. **BESCOM (P).** The electricity supply company for the city and surrounding districts.
132. **BWSSB (P).** The city's water supply and sewerage board, which supplies Cauvery water and runs sewers.
133. **Cauvery connection (P).** A piped BWSSB water connection. Ask whether it's in place or only planned.
134. **Covered parking (O).** A parking slot under a roof or in a basement.
135. **DG set (M).** The diesel generator for power backup.
136. **Gated community (O).** A project with controlled entry and shared amenities.
137. **Handover (O).** The formal transfer of the flat, or of common areas to the association.
138. **Open parking (O).** An uncovered parking slot.
139. **Plot, or site (O).** A piece of land for building. In Bengaluru, "site" usually means a residential plot.
140. **Rainwater harvesting (P).** Collecting rainwater for use or recharge, mandatory in Bengaluru for many buildings.
141. **Ready-to-move (M).** A completed flat with an OC. No GST applies.
142. **Resale (O).** Buying from an existing owner rather than the builder.
143. **Row house (O).** Houses sharing side walls, built in a row.
144. **Snag list (M).** Your written list of defects found at inspection.
145. **STP (P).** Sewage treatment plant, required in larger projects.
146. **Tower (O).** One building in a multi-building project.
147. **Under-construction (M).** A flat not yet complete. GST and stage-linked payments apply.
148. **Unit (O).** A single flat, apartment or shop.
149. **Villa (O).** A standalone house in a project, often with UDS in the land.
150. **Water meter (M).** A meter for each flat, which helps share water costs fairly.

## Checklist: terms to confirm in your own documents

- [ ] RERA carpet area matches across agreement, sale deed and RERA filing
- [ ] UDS is stated in the sale deed, with the method
- [ ] EC covers 30 years (Form 15 or 16)
- [ ] Khata type (A or B) and status (draft or final e-khata) are known
- [ ] "CC" is clarified: commencement or completion
- [ ] OC is issued for your tower if the flat is complete
- [ ] Guidance value checked on Kaveri
- [ ] GST rate (5%, 1% or nil) confirmed
- [ ] Mortgage on the project, and the lender's NOC, confirmed
- [ ] Delay interest clause matches section 18

## What mistakes do buyers make with property terms?

- **Treating e-khata as a new khata type.** It's a digital record of either A or B.
- **Confusing the two CCs.** A commencement certificate isn't a completion certificate.
- **Comparing super built-up prices.** Use carpet area.
- **Assuming mutation proves title.** It doesn't; the title chain does.
- **Ignoring UDS.** It's your share of the land.
- **Taking GPA sales at face value.** A GPA alone doesn't transfer ownership.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is UDS in a flat?

UDS is your undivided share of the land under the building. It's usually worked out in proportion to your flat's area and should be stated in your sale deed.

### What's the difference between carpet area and super built-up area?

Carpet area is the usable floor inside your flat, as defined in RERA. Super built-up area adds walls and your share of common areas. The gap is the loading.

### What is an encumbrance certificate?

A record from Kaveri of registered transactions on a property, such as sales and mortgages, for a period you choose. Form 16 means none were found.

### What is the difference between A-Khata and B-Khata?

A-Khata is the register for properties that comply with approvals. B-Khata records properties with approval or bye-law gaps. Loans and resale are easier with A-Khata.

### What is guidance value in Karnataka?

The government's minimum value per unit area for a property. Stamp duty is paid on the higher of the price and the guidance value.

### What does CC mean in property?

It can mean commencement certificate (permission to start building) or completion certificate (confirmation the building is complete). Always ask which one.

### What is an occupancy certificate?

Permission to occupy a building, issued after the authority checks it against the sanctioned plan. Don't take possession of a new flat without one.

### What is a JDA in real estate?

A joint development agreement, where a landowner provides land and a builder develops it, and the flats are split between them. Check whose share your flat is in.
