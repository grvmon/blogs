# Living in Yeshwanthpur: rates, metro, water and flood risk

**Short answer:** Yeshwanthpur property rates were ₹7,700 to ₹14,500 per sq ft in H1 2026, up 9% in a year, according to Knight Frank. Guidance values run from ₹1,30,000 per sq m on Tumkur Road to ₹45,000 in Baiyanna Layout, with apartment lines at ₹75,000 to ₹1,00,000. The Green Line stops at Yeshwanthpur.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **Market range:** ₹7,700 to ₹14,500 per sq ft, up 9% in 12 months and 6% in six (Knight Frank, H1 2026).
> - **Guidance values:** ₹1,10,000 per sq m on the main roads, ₹70,000 for the core Yeshwanthpur line and ₹45,000 in the inner layouts.
> - **Metro running:** the Green Line has a station at Yeshwanthpur, with suburban rail Corridor-2 targeted for December 2028.
> - **Inside the city:** PIN 560022 is fully in the West City Corporation.
> - **Our RAIL check:** railway noise, APMC and truck traffic, industrial zoning, line access.

Yeshwanthpur is a north-west Bengaluru area at the city end of Tumkur Road. It has a major railway junction, the APMC market yard and the Industrial Suburb, alongside older residential streets and apartment blocks. This guide uses official records and named sources, and says where data runs out.

![Map of Yeshwanthpur showing the railway junction, Tumkur Road, the Industrial Suburb, Mathikere and the Green Line station](/img/blog/yeshwanthpur-map.png)

## Who governs Yeshwanthpur?

The West City Corporation. All of PIN 560022 is in the West corporation, covering the Goraguntepalya, Peenya and Nalwadi Krishnaraja Wadiyar wards. It's inside GBA, so stamp duty, cess, surcharge and registration add up to 7.6%. Mathikere, to the east, is PIN 560054, split between the North and West corporations, so check yours with our [ward and corporation lookup](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/which-ward-and-city-corporation-is-my-flat-in-gba-pin-code-lookup/).

## What are property rates in Yeshwanthpur?

### Market prices

Knight Frank's H1 2026 report put Yeshwanthpur at ₹7,700 to ₹14,500 per sq ft, up 9% in 12 months and 6% in six ([Knight Frank](https://content.knightfrank.com/research/3116/documents/en/india-real-estate-office-and-residential-market-h1-2026-12927.pdf)). That's below Malleswaram (₹9,200 to ₹17,500) and Rajajinagar (₹11,500 to ₹23,200) in the same report.

### Official guidance values

These come from the 2023 gazette (Rajajinagar book). Divide by 10.764 for per sq ft.

## Is there a metro in Yeshwanthpur?

Yes. The Green Line runs along Tumkur Road through Yeshwanthpur, with the line extended to Madavara in November 2024 ([The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/karnataka/bengalurus-namma-metro-green-line-extends-to-madavara), news).

| Line | Nearby stations | Status, 19 Oct 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Green Line | Yeshwanthpur, Sandal Soap Factory, Goraguntepalya (station names per news reports) | Running |
| Suburban rail Corridor-2 | Chikkabanavara–Yeshwanthpur (7.4 km) and Yeshwanthpur–Benniganahalli (17.5 km) | Target December 2028 (Knight Frank, citing K-RIDE) |
| Phase 3 Orange Line | Peenya interchange on the Green Line | Approved August 2024, PIB target 2029 |

Suburban rail dates can change, so don't price a flat on the 2028 date. Our [metro lines guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-metro-lines-2026-status-of-every-line-for-homebuyers/) covers the rest.

## Where does Yeshwanthpur get its water?

Yeshwanthpur is one of the eight old zones covered by Cauvery Stage 5, which targets villages added to the city in 2007, not the older core streets. By May 2026, about 1.15 lakh of an estimated 1.8 lakh buildings in those villages were connected ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/bengalurus-water-woes-piped-connections-sanchari-cauvery-reduce-tanker-demand/article70978412.ece), news). Ask for the building's BWSSB connection and recent bills. Our [Cauvery Stage 5 guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/cauvery-stage-5-coverage-which-areas-get-bwssb-water/) explains how.

## Is Yeshwanthpur flood-prone?

We didn't find a 2026 official list naming Yeshwanthpur locations. In April 2026, the West City Corporation had 48 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations, the most of any corporation ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/bengaluru/2026/Apr/11/52-of-101-flood-prone-locations-in-bengaluru-yet-to-be-fixed-before-monsoon), news). That figure covers the whole corporation, not this area.

Railway underpasses and low streets can hold water after heavy rain, so check the approach road too. See our [lake and drain checks](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/flood-risk-lake-buffers-and-rajakaluve-checks-before-you-buy/) and [flood-prone areas map](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-flood-prone-areas-map-from-ksndmc-data/).

At the low end, the price is only 11% above the guidance value. If the flat's row on Kaveri is higher, or floor and parking amounts are added, it could tip over. Check the exact row before you agree a price.

## The RAIL check for Yeshwanthpur (our framework)

| Check | Question | Where to check | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Railway noise | How close is the flat to the junction, yards or tracks? | Visit at night | Bedrooms facing the tracks |
| APMC and trucks | Is the street on a market or lorry route? | Visit early morning | Trucks parked on your approach road |
| Industrial zoning | Is the plot residential, or in the Industrial Suburb? | Master plan, conversion order | Flat inside an industrial zone |
| Line access | How long to the Green Line station? | Walk it at peak hour | Crossing Tumkur Road with no foot overbridge |

Score each check green, amber or red. Two reds and you should walk away or renegotiate. Our [legal checks guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-property-legal-checks-from-title-to-oc/) covers conversion and title.

## Yeshwanthpur buyer's checklist

- [ ] Confirm the ward and khata.
- [ ] Find the exact guidance value row on Kaveri.
- [ ] Work out the price-to-guidance ratio.
- [ ] Check land use and any conversion order.
- [ ] Visit at night and early morning for rail and truck noise.
- [ ] Time the walk to the Green Line station.
- [ ] Ask for BWSSB bills and tanker spend.
- [ ] Review RERA filings for a new project.

## What mistakes do buyers make in Yeshwanthpur?

- **Using one rate for the area.** Guidance values nearly triple from inner layouts to Tumkur Road.
- **Visiting only in the afternoon.** Rail and market traffic peak at other times.
- **Pricing in suburban rail.** The December 2028 target isn't a promise.
- **Skipping zoning checks.** The Industrial Suburb sits beside housing.
