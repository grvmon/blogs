# Hennur vs Thanisandra: the data side by side

**Short answer:** Hennur vs Thanisandra is a close call on price. Knight Frank puts Hennur at ₹7,000–14,000 per sq ft (+10% in a year) and Thanisandra at ₹5,700–13,500 (+6%). Neither has a running metro; the Blue Line's KR Puram–Hebbal stretch is expected by December 2027 to March 2028. Only Thanisandra has a published rent figure.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **Price:** at the midpoints, a 1,000 sq ft flat costs ₹9 lakh more in Hennur (Knight Frank H1 2026).
> - **Rent:** ANAROCK puts Thanisandra Main Road 2BHK rents at ₹28,000–42,000 a month; it publishes none for Hennur.
> - **Metro:** both wait for the Blue Line (news). Hennur is also a planned station on suburban Corridor-4.
> - **Floors:** main-road guidance values are almost the same, ₹78,000 and ₹80,000 per sq m.
> - **Our PATH test:** pocket, arterial, transit, holding cost.

Hennur and Thanisandra sit next to each other north of the outer ring road, both inside GBA, both feeding Manyata Tech Park. This page puts our [Hennur guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/living-in-hennur-property-rates-metro-water-flood-risk/) and [Thanisandra guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/living-in-thanisandra-property-rates-metro-water-flood-risk/) side by side.

## How do Hennur and Thanisandra compare?

| Measure | Hennur | Thanisandra |
|---|---|---|
| Knight Frank range, H1 2026 | ₹7,000–14,000 per sq ft | ₹5,700–13,500 per sq ft |
| 12-month change | +10% | +6% |
| ANAROCK base rate, Q1 2026 | Not published | ₹10,100 per sq ft (Thanisandra Main Road) |
| 2BHK rent | Not published | ₹28,000–42,000 a month |
| Site guidance values (₹/sq m) | ₹25,000 to ₹80,000 (₹1,50,000 on the ring road) | ₹28,500 to ₹78,000 |
| Metro | Blue Line, expected Dec 2027 to Mar 2028 (news) | Blue Line at Nagawara, same window; Pink Line to Nagawara delayed (news) |
| Suburban rail | Station on Corridor-4, no completion date | None in the area |
| Flood record | Sai Layout, next door, flooded again in May 2026 (news) | Check low pockets near Rachenahalli lake |
| Stamp charges | 7.6% (inside GBA) | 7.6% (inside GBA) |

Guidance values are the 2023 rates still in force; check Kaveri for your date, as our [guidance value table](https://acrenkey.com/insights/bengaluru-property-rates-by-area-guidance-value-table-2026/) explains.

## Is Hennur or Thanisandra cheaper?

Thanisandra, slightly. Its range starts ₹1,300 per sq ft lower and ends ₹500 lower. Hennur grew faster over the last year, +10% against +6% ([Knight Frank H1 2026](https://content.knightfrank.com/research/3116/documents/en/india-real-estate-office-and-residential-market-h1-2026-12927.pdf)).

Official floors overlap. Hennur village is ₹50,000 per sq m and the Hennur–Bagaluru main road ₹80,000. Thanisandra Main Road is ₹78,000, with Hegde Nagar behind it at ₹33,000.

## Which has better metro access?

Neither has a running station. The Blue Line's KR Puram–Hebbal stretch, with stations including HBR Layout, Kalyan Nagar and Nagawara (station names per news reports), is expected by December 2027 to March 2028 ([Times of India, 9 Oct 2026](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/bengaluru/bengaluru-metro-blue-line-delayed-to-march-2028-how-the-58km-airport-corridor-will-open-in-phases-which-stretches-will-be-ready-first-and-when-commuters-can-expect-services-from-central-silk-board-to-kia/photostory/134829302.cms), news). Nagawara will also be the Pink Line's northern end, but that underground stretch has no firm date.

Hennur adds a planned suburban rail station on Corridor-4 ([K-RIDE Samparka, May 2026](https://kride.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/SAMPARKA-MAY-2026-ISSUE-VOLUME-10.pdf)), with no revised completion date. Our [metro lines guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-metro-lines-2026-status-of-every-line-for-homebuyers/) tracks both.

## Which has better water supply?

They're in the same position. Both include villages added to the city in 2007 and covered by Cauvery Stage 5. By May 2026, about 1.15 lakh of an estimated 1.8 lakh buildings in those villages were connected ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/bengalurus-water-woes-piped-connections-sanchari-cauvery-reduce-tanker-demand/article70978412.ece), news). Ask for the BWSSB connection and bills; our [Cauvery Stage 5 guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/cauvery-stage-5-coverage-which-areas-get-bwssb-water/) shows how.

## Which is more flood-prone?

Hennur's side has the fresher record. Sai Layout, next to Hennur, flooded again on 18 May 2026, along with homes beside drains in Kalyan Nagar and Horamavu ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/bengaluru/2026/May/19/homes-in-east-bengaluru-in-a-mess-sai-layout-bears-brunt-again), news). In Thanisandra, low pockets near Rachenahalli lake and the valley drains need checking. Use our [lake and drain checks](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/flood-risk-lake-buffers-and-rajakaluve-checks-before-you-buy/) for the site.

## Worked examples

These use published figures and illustrative assumptions.

### Worked example 1: a 1,000 sq ft flat at each midpoint

| Step | Hennur (₹10,500) | Thanisandra (₹9,600) |
|---|---|---|
| Price | ₹1,05,00,000 | ₹96,00,000 |
| Charges at 7.6% | ₹7,98,000 | ₹7,29,600 |
| 80% loan | ₹84,00,000 | ₹76,80,000 |
| EMI, 20 years at 8.5% | ₹72,897 | ₹66,649 |

The gap is ₹9 lakh on price, ₹68,400 on charges and ₹6,248 a month on the EMI.

### Worked example 2: the rent Hennur would need

Thanisandra's gross yield, using ANAROCK's figures: a 1,000 sq ft flat at ₹10,100 is ₹1,01,00,000. The midpoint rent of ₹35,000 a month gives ₹4,20,000 a year, or about 4.2%.

To match that yield at Hennur's midpoint price of ₹1,05,00,000, a flat would need about ₹36,386 a month. There's no published Hennur rent to test that against, so ask for recent rental agreements in the building. Our [rent vs buy page](https://answers.acrenkey.com/rent-vs-buy-bengaluru/) covers the other side of the decision.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Hennur more expensive than Thanisandra?

Slightly. Knight Frank's H1 2026 ranges are ₹7,000–14,000 per sq ft for Hennur and ₹5,700–13,500 for Thanisandra. At the midpoints, a 1,000 sq ft flat costs ₹9 lakh more in Hennur.

### Which has grown faster?

Hennur, at +10% over 12 months against +6% for Thanisandra (Knight Frank H1 2026). One year isn't a trend, so don't extrapolate it.

### When will the metro reach Hennur and Thanisandra?

The Blue Line's KR Puram–Hebbal stretch is expected by December 2027 to March 2028, per news reports. Neither area has a running station today.

### What rent can I get?

ANAROCK puts Thanisandra Main Road 2BHK rents at ₹28,000–42,000 a month. It publishes no Hennur figure, so ask for recent agreements in the building.

### Which is more flood-prone?

Sai Layout, next to Hennur, flooded again in May 2026. In Thanisandra, check low pockets near Rachenahalli lake. Judge the specific site, not the area.

### Do both get Cauvery water?

Both include villages being connected through Cauvery Stage 5. Supply varies building by building, so ask for the BWSSB connection and bills.

### Are both inside GBA?

Yes. Records are e-Khata on e-Aasthi, and stamp charges total 7.6% of the higher of price and guidance value. On a ₹96 lakh flat, that's ₹7,29,600. Try our [stamp duty calculator](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/stamp-duty-and-registration-calculator-karnataka/).

### Is the Hennur suburban rail station worth paying for?

Not yet. Corridor-4 has no revised completion date, so treat it as a possible future benefit, not part of the price you pay today.
