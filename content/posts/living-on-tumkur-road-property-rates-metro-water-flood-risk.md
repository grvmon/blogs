# Tumkur Road property rates (2026): metro, water and flood risk

**Short answer:** Tumkur Road property rates were ₹5,300 to ₹10,200 per sq ft in H1 2026, up 16% in a year, according to Knight Frank. ANAROCK put the average launch value at ₹9,200. Guidance values run from ₹2,00,000 per sq m on the highway frontage to ₹40,000 in Chikkabidarakallu. The Green Line runs along the road.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **Market range:** ₹5,300 to ₹10,200 per sq ft, up 16% in 12 months and 18% in six (Knight Frank, H1 2026).
> - **Launch figure:** ₹9,200 per sq ft average in Q4 2025, with 1,000 units launched in 2021–2025 (ANAROCK).
> - **Guidance values:** ₹2,00,000 per sq m on the highway, ₹70,000 in Dasarahalli and ₹60,000 in Peenya.
> - **Metro running:** the Green Line runs from Yeshwanthpur to Madavara along the road.
> - **Our LANE check:** lorry noise, access to the metro, notified use, edge of the city.

Tumkur Road (Tumakuru Road, NH-48) runs north-west from Yeshwanthpur past Peenya, Jalahalli Cross, Dasarahalli and Nagasandra towards Nelamangala. It mixes industrial estates, older layouts and newer apartment projects. For ground checks on communities along the highway, see our review of [Brigade Lumina](https://acrenkey.com/property/brigade-lumina-tumkur-road/). ## Who governs Tumkur Road?

Two corporations. Yeshwanthpur's PIN 560022 is fully West, and 560073 (Nagasandra, Dodda Bidarakallu) is 88% West. Peenya's 560057 is North, and 560058 is split West and North. About 12% of 560073 lies outside GBA, and land past Madavara towards Nelamangala is outside it. Inside GBA, stamp duty, cess, surcharge and registration add up to 7.6%; outside, 7.65%. Check your plot with our [ward and corporation lookup](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/which-ward-and-city-corporation-is-my-flat-in-gba-pin-code-lookup/).

## What are property rates on Tumkur Road?

### Market prices

Knight Frank's H1 2026 report put Tumkur Road at ₹5,300 to ₹10,200 per sq ft. That's up 16% in 12 months and 18% in six, the fastest 12-month rise of the four West locations it tracks ([Knight Frank](https://content.knightfrank.com/research/3116/documents/en/india-real-estate-office-and-residential-market-h1-2026-12927.pdf)). ANAROCK's April 2026 report put the average launch value at ₹9,200 per sq ft in Q4 2025, with 1,000 units launched in 2021–2025, 9% of its West-zone launches ([ANAROCK](https://assets.credai.app/public/knowledge_center_document/1775212502300_Anarock_Bengaluru_Where_Talent_Meets_Capital_003eec8ec3.pdf)).

## Is there a metro on Tumkur Road?

Yes. The Green Line runs above the road from Yeshwanthpur to Madavara. Its extension from Nagasandra to Madavara opened in November 2024 ([The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/karnataka/bengalurus-namma-metro-green-line-extends-to-madavara), news).

| Line | Stations along the road (station names per news reports) | Status, 19 Oct 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Green Line | Yeshwanthpur, Goraguntepalya, Peenya Industry, Peenya, Jalahalli, Dasarahalli, Nagasandra, Manjunathanagar, Chikkabidarakallu, Madavara | Running |
| Phase 3 Orange Line | Peenya (interchange), Kanteerava Nagar, BEL Circle (per BMRCL's DPR) | Approved August 2024, PIB target 2029 |

The Orange Line will cross Tumkur Road at Peenya on the outer ring road ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2046081)). Most Green Line stations sit right on the highway, so your walk depends on how far your gate is from the road. Our [metro lines guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-metro-lines-2026-status-of-every-line-for-homebuyers/) covers the rest.

## Where does Tumkur Road get its water?

The Dasarahalli and Yeshwanthpur zones are among the eight old zones covered by Cauvery Stage 5, built for villages added to the city in 2007. By May 2026, about 1.15 lakh of an estimated 1.8 lakh buildings in those villages were connected ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/bengalurus-water-woes-piped-connections-sanchari-cauvery-reduce-tanker-demand/article70978412.ece), news). Projects beyond the GBA line may rely on borewells and tankers. Ask for the BWSSB connection and recent bills. Our [Cauvery Stage 5 guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/cauvery-stage-5-coverage-which-areas-get-bwssb-water/) explains how.

## Is Tumkur Road flood-prone?

We didn't find a 2026 official list naming Tumkur Road locations. In April 2026, the West City Corporation had 48 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations, the most of any corporation, and the North had 6 ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/bengaluru/2026/Apr/11/52-of-101-flood-prone-locations-in-bengaluru-yet-to-be-fixed-before-monsoon), news). That figure covers the whole corporation, not this road.

Underpasses and service roads along the highway can hold water after heavy rain, so check your street, not just the road. See our [lake and drain checks](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/flood-risk-lake-buffers-and-rajakaluve-checks-before-you-buy/) and [flood-prone areas map](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-flood-prone-areas-map-from-ksndmc-data/).

## Worked examples

These use published figures and illustrative prices.

### Worked example 1: a flat at both ends of Knight Frank's range

A 1,200 sq ft flat on a ₹64,000 per sq m apartment line (guidance value ₹71,34,894).

| Price per sq ft | Price | Price ÷ guidance value | Charges at 7.6% | 110% of price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₹5,300 (low) | ₹63,60,000 | 0.89 | ₹5,42,252 (on guidance value) | ₹69,96,000 |
| ₹10,200 (high) | ₹1,22,40,000 | 1.72 | ₹9,30,240 | ₹1,34,64,000 |

At the low end, the guidance value is above the price, so you pay ₹58,892 more in charges than on the price alone. It's also above 110% of the price, so the income-tax rules on the gap apply. Ask a CA before you sign.

### Worked example 2: a 1,200 sq ft site on three lines

| Line | Guidance value (₹/sq m) | Site value | Charges at 7.6% |
|---|---|---|---|
| Highway frontage | 2,00,000 | ₹2,22,96,544 | ₹16,94,537 |
| Dasarahalli | 70,000 | ₹78,03,790 | ₹5,93,088 |
| Chikkabidarakallu | 40,000 | ₹44,59,309 | ₹3,38,907 |

The highway frontage is valued five times the Chikkabidarakallu line. A seller's "on Tumkur Road" can mean either.

## The LANE check for Tumkur Road (our framework)

| Check | Question | Where to check | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lorry noise | How close is the flat to the highway and truck routes? | Visit at night and at peak hour | Bedrooms facing the highway with no buffer |

- [ ] Review RERA filings for a new project.

## What mistakes do buyers make on Tumkur Road?

- **Using one rate for the whole road.** Guidance values vary five-fold.
- **Ignoring the 110% test on cheaper flats.** Low prices can fall below the guidance value.
- **Not checking zoning.** Industrial estates sit beside housing.
- **Assuming every address is inside GBA.** Part of 560073 and the stretch past Madavara are outside.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are Tumkur Road property rates in 2026?

Knight Frank put Tumkur Road at ₹5,300 to ₹10,200 per sq ft in H1 2026, up 16% in a year. ANAROCK's average launch value was ₹9,200 in Q4 2025. Guidance values range from ₹40,000 to ₹2,00,000 per sq m.

### Is Tumkur Road good to live in?

It suits buyers working in the north-west or along the Green Line, who want lower prices than central areas. The trade-offs are highway traffic and noise, industrial neighbours and uneven water supply further out. Use the LANE check before you choose.

### Is there a metro on Tumkur Road?

Yes. The Green Line runs along it from Yeshwanthpur to Madavara, with stations including Peenya, Dasarahalli and Nagasandra (station names per news reports). The Madavara extension opened in November 2024.

### What is the guidance value on Tumkur Road?

It depends on the row. The highway frontage is ₹2,00,000 per sq m, Dasarahalli ₹70,000, Peenya ₹60,000 and Chikkabidarakallu ₹40,000. Many apartment lines are ₹58,000 to ₹64,000.

### Does Tumkur Road get Cauvery water?

The Dasarahalli and Yeshwanthpur zones are covered by Cauvery Stage 5, where about 1.15 lakh of 1.8 lakh buildings were connected by May 2026. Projects further out may rely on borewells and tankers.

### Is Tumkur Road flood-prone?

We didn't find an official 2026 list naming Tumkur Road points. The West corporation had 48 of 101 listed locations. Check underpasses, service roads and drains near the property.

### How much stamp duty will I pay on Tumkur Road?

Inside GBA, charges total 7.6% of the higher of price and guidance value; outside, 7.65%. On a ₹1.22 crore flat inside GBA, that's ₹9,30,240. Try our [stamp duty calculator](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/stamp-duty-and-registration-calculator-karnataka/).

### Which areas are near Tumkur Road?

Yeshwanthpur sits at its city end, with Peenya, Jalahalli and Dasarahalli along it. See our [Yeshwanthpur guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/living-in-yeshwanthpur-property-rates-metro-water-flood-risk/).
