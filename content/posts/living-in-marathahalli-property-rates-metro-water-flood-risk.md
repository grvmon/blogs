# Marathahalli property rates (2026): metro, water and flood risk

**Short answer:** Marathahalli property rates for flats run ₹7,310 to ₹15,500 per sq ft (Knight Frank, H1 2026), up 15% in a year, the fourth-fastest rise of 17 Bengaluru locations. Official guidance values are high too: ₹84,187 per sq m for the village site line and ₹1,20,150 on the ring road. The Blue Line station is still being built.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **Prices are rising fast:** +15% in 12 months, behind only KR Puram, Yelahanka and Tumkur Road.
> - **A high floor:** the village site line works out to ₹7,821 per sq ft, above Knight Frank's low end for flats.
> - **Metro soon:** Marathahalli is on the Blue Line's Silk Board–KR Puram section, now targeted for mid-2027 (news), and on suburban Corridor-4.
> - **Flood points nearby:** the East corporation had 20 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations in April 2026 (news).
> - **Our RING check:** rail, inundation, notified line, gridlock.

Marathahalli grew up around the outer ring road bridge and the tech parks that line the ring road on both sides. It sits between Whitefield, KR Puram, Bellandur and the old airport road. If you are evaluating buy-to-let properties near ring road tech campuses, test cashflows with our [rental yield calculator](https://acrenkey.com/calculators/rental-yield/). This guide sticks to official records and named sources.

## Who governs Marathahalli?

Marathahalli is inside the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) area, so records are e-Khata on e-Aasthi and charges are about 7.6% of value. GBA's five city corporations and 369 wards don't follow pin codes neatly. Use our [ward and corporation lookup](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/which-ward-and-city-corporation-is-my-flat-in-gba-pin-code-lookup/), then confirm from the tax receipt.

## What are property rates in Marathahalli?

### Official guidance values

Residential site rates from the 2023 gazette (in force since 1 Oct 2023), Shivajinagar registration-district book:

| Gazette line (residential site) | Per sq m | Per sq ft |
|---|---|---|
| Marathahalli Main Road | ₹65,000 | ₹6,039 |
| Marathahalli (village) | ₹84,187 | ₹7,821 |
| Marathahalli–Varthur Main Road (80 ft road) | ₹95,904 | ₹8,910 |
| Ring Road–Marathahalli | ₹1,20,150 | ₹11,162 |
| Apartment lines in the Marathahalli section (several projects) | ₹63,800 for most, one at ₹84,150 | ₹5,927–7,818 |

The Marathahalli Main Road line is listed in a different section of the book from the village lines, so confirm which applies to your survey number. Flats are notified project by project, so we don't name them. Look up your own on Kaveri, and compare localities in our [rates-by-area table](https://acrenkey.com/insights/bengaluru-property-rates-by-area-guidance-value-table-2026/). A revision has been reported in the news, but none had been notified as of 19 Oct 2026.

### Market prices

Knight Frank's H1 2026 report puts Marathahalli flat prices at **₹7,310 to ₹15,500 per sq ft**, up **15%** over 12 months. Knight Frank counts Marathahalli in East Bengaluru, with Whitefield, KR Puram, Old Airport Road and Old Madras Road. These are consultant figures for new stock, not registered sales. For one building, check past registered values on Kaveri encumbrance certificates.

### Rents

ANAROCK lists Marathahalli in its East zone but gives no rent or base rate for it, and listing-portal rents aren't evidence. For nearby comparison, ANAROCK quotes ₹30,000 to ₹45,000 a month for a 1,000 sq ft 2BHK in Whitefield in Q1 2026 (see our [Whitefield guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/living-in-whitefield-property-rates-metro-water-flood-risk/)).

### What's being built

Marathahalli falls in Bengaluru East taluk on Karnataka RERA. In our [East RERA study](https://acrenkey.com/insights/do-east-bengaluru-projects-deliver-on-their-rera-date/), the taluk had 1,235 approved housing and plotted projects, and 63.1% still carried their original completion date. The taluk also covers Whitefield, KR Puram and Mahadevapura, so these aren't Marathahalli-only figures.

## Is there a metro in Marathahalli?

Not yet. Two rail lines are being built through Marathahalli:

| Line | What it does | Status (Oct 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Blue Line Phase 2A, Silk Board–KR Puram (19.75 km) | Runs along the ring road with a Marathahalli station (station names per news reports), linking to the Purple Line at KR Puram and the Yellow Line at Silk Board | Targeted for mid-2027, per the Bengaluru development minister ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/bangalore/blue-line-to-open-in-phases-from-mid-2027-full-stretch-by-march-2028-says-kbg/article71378865.ece), news). In August 2026, work at the Marathahalli station was still incomplete ([News18, citing Deccan Herald](https://www.news18.com/cities/bengaluru-news/bengaluru-metro-blue-line-trial-runs-to-start-in-october-but-2-stations-face-delay-ws-l-10281524.html), news) |
| Suburban Corridor-4 (Kanaka), Heelalige–Rajanukunte | Marathahalli is the only elevated station in the southern package | Station works under way in the package; no revised completion date for the corridor |

Suburban details are from K-RIDE's May 2026 newsletter and the government's reply to Lok Sabha question 583 of 22 Jul 2026. Our [KR Puram guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/living-in-kr-puram-property-rates-metro-water-flood-risk/) covers the Purple and Blue Line interchange.

## Where does Marathahalli get its water?

The Marathahalli belt falls in the former Mahadevapura zone, one of eight zones covered by Cauvery Stage 5, which serves villages added to the city in 2007 ([JICA](https://www.jica.go.jp/english/overseas/india/information/press/2024/1553733_53431.html)). By May 2026, about 1.15 lakh of an estimated 1.8 lakh buildings in those villages were connected ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/bengalurus-water-woes-piped-connections-sanchari-cauvery-reduce-tanker-demand/article70978412.ece), news). So supply varies building by building. Ask for the BWSSB connection details and recent bills. Our [Cauvery Stage 5 guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/cauvery-stage-5-coverage-which-areas-get-bwssb-water/) shows how.

## Is Marathahalli flood-prone?

Some points nearby are. In April 2026, the East corporation had 20 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations, and 52 of the 101 citywide were still unfixed ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/bengaluru/2026/Apr/11/52-of-101-flood-prone-locations-in-bengaluru-yet-to-be-fixed-before-monsoon), news). GBA's 2026 pre-monsoon circular named Panathur 'S' Cross, just south-east of Marathahalli, among the locations flagged ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/gba-directs-five-corporations-to-step-up-pre-monsoon-preparedness/article70816622.ece), news).

Points are roads, not buildings. The belt has many lakes and drains, so check the site itself. Lake buffers became size-based (0 to 30 m) under Act 19 of 2026, and primary drains carry a 50 m buffer under RMP-2015. Our [lake and drain checks](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/flood-risk-lake-buffers-and-rajakaluve-checks-before-you-buy/) show how.

## Worked examples

### Worked example 1: a new flat close to the floor

Illustrative. A 1,200 sq ft flat at Knight Frank's low end of ₹7,310 per sq ft, on a ₹63,800 per sq m apartment line.

| Item | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Price | 1,200 × ₹7,310 | ₹87,72,000 |
| Guidance value | 1,200 ÷ 10.764 × ₹63,800 | ₹71,12,598 |
| Price ÷ guidance value | ₹87,72,000 ÷ ₹71,12,598 | 1.23 |
| Charges at 7.6% | 7.6% × ₹87,72,000 | ₹6,66,672 |

The gap is narrower than in north Bengaluru. Kaveri can add floor, amenity and parking amounts to a flat's value, which could push the official value above a low price, so check the exact figure first.

### Worked example 2: an older resale flat priced below the floor

Illustrative. A 1,000 sq ft resale flat on the same line, agreed at ₹5,500 per sq ft.

| Item | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Price | 1,000 × ₹5,500 | ₹55,00,000 |
| Guidance value | 1,000 ÷ 10.764 × ₹63,800 | ₹59,27,165 |
| Charges at 7.6% on the guidance value | 7.6% × ₹59,27,165 | ₹4,50,465 |
| Extra vs charges on the price | ₹4,50,465 − ₹4,18,000 | ₹32,465 |

Budget charges on ₹59.27 lakh. The guidance value is within 110% of the price (₹60.5 lakh), so the income-tax gap rules in our [guidance value vs market price post](https://acrenkey.com/insights/guidance-value-vs-market-price-east-bengaluru/) shouldn't be triggered. A slightly lower price would trigger them. Confirm with your CA.

### Worked example 3: a 1,200 sq ft site on three lines

Illustrative.

| Gazette line | Guidance value | Minimum charges at 7.6% |
|---|---|---|
| Marathahalli village, ₹84,187 per sq m | ₹93,85,396 | ₹7,13,290 |
| Marathahalli–Varthur Main Road, ₹95,904 | ₹1,06,91,639 | ₹8,12,565 |
| Ring Road–Marathahalli, ₹1,20,150 | ₹1,33,94,649 | ₹10,17,993 |

A ring road site carries a floor 1.43 times the village line. Our [full cost guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/full-cost-bengaluru-flat-stamp-duty-registration-gst/) adds GST and other costs for flats.

## What mistakes do buyers make in Marathahalli?

- **Assuming duty always goes on the price.** Guidance values here are high, so low-priced and older flats can be valued above the agreed price.
- **Counting the station before it opens.** The Marathahalli station was still incomplete in August 2026.
- **Judging flood risk by the area name.** Flood points are specific roads and junctions.
- **Using one rate for the whole area.** The ring road line is much higher than the village line behind it.

*This is general information, not legal or tax advice. Ask a property lawyer to check title and approvals, and a CA about the tax side.*

## Frequently asked questions

### What are property rates in Marathahalli in 2026?

Knight Frank's H1 2026 report puts Marathahalli flat prices at ₹7,310 to ₹15,500 per sq ft, up 15% in a year. Official site guidance values run from ₹84,187 per sq m for the village line to ₹1,20,150 on the ring road, and most apartment lines are ₹63,800.

### Is Marathahalli good to live in?

It suits people working at ring road tech parks who want a short commute. Against that, traffic on the ring road is heavy, the metro isn't running yet, and some nearby points flood. Run the rail, inundation, notified line, gridlock check on each property before deciding.

### Is there a metro in Marathahalli?

Not yet. Marathahalli is a station on the Blue Line's Silk Board–KR Puram section (station names per news reports), which the minister said in October 2026 would likely open by mid-2027. Work at the Marathahalli station was still incomplete in August 2026. It's also a station on suburban Corridor-4.

### Does Marathahalli get Cauvery water?

Many buildings do, but not all. The Marathahalli belt is in the former Mahadevapura zone covered by Cauvery Stage 5, where about 1.15 lakh of 1.8 lakh buildings were connected by May 2026. Ask for the building's BWSSB connection details and recent bills.

### Is Marathahalli flood-prone?

Some nearby roads and junctions are. In April 2026 the East corporation had 20 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations, and GBA flagged Panathur 'S' Cross. Check the specific site, the nearest drain and lake, and visit after heavy rain before you commit.

### What is the guidance value in Marathahalli?

Under the 2023 gazette, the Marathahalli village site line is ₹84,187 per sq m, Marathahalli–Varthur Main Road ₹95,904 and Ring Road–Marathahalli ₹1,20,150. Most apartment lines in the section are ₹63,800 per sq m. Check your exact property on Kaveri.

### How much stamp duty will I pay in Marathahalli?

About 7.6% of the price: 5% duty, 10% cess and 2% surcharge on the duty, plus 2% registration. On an ₹87.72 lakh flat that's ₹6,66,672. Guidance values here are high, so check whether the official value is above your price.

### What rent can I expect in Marathahalli?

We don't have a reliable figure for Marathahalli itself. ANAROCK lists it in the East zone without a rent, and portal rents are asking prices. Nearby Whitefield's 2BHK rent was ₹30,000 to ₹45,000 a month in Q1 2026 (ANAROCK). Ask for recent agreements in the building.
