# Should you buy a home now or wait? Signs for Bengaluru buyers

**Short answer:** Whether to buy a home now or wait in Bengaluru depends more on you than the market. In October 2026 the RBI raised repo to 5.50% and signalled no near-term cuts, prices rose 9% in a year, and unsold stock is growing. If you're financially ready and staying five years or more, waiting for a dip is a gamble.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **Rates turned:** the RBI raised repo by 25 bps on 7 Oct 2026 and said rate cuts are off the table in the near term.
> - **Prices are still rising:** Knight Frank's Bengaluru average was ₹9,354 per sq ft in H1 2026, up 9% in a year.
> - **Stock is building:** ANAROCK counted 72,800 unsold units in Q1 2026, up 24% in a year.
> - **A rate cut is easily cancelled:** a 3.75% price rise wipes out the EMI saving from a 0.5-point cut.
> - **Our READY test** tells you whether you're ready, whatever the market does.

Nobody can time a property market reliably, including us. What you can do is read the current signals honestly and check whether your own situation says buy or wait. If you are buying jointly, work through our [25 questions for couples before buying a home](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/25-questions-every-couple-should-discuss-before-buying-a-home/).

## What are the market signals saying in October 2026?

| Signal | Latest reading | Leans towards |
|---|---|---|
| Repo rate | Raised to 5.50% on 7 Oct 2026; "rate cuts are off the table in the near term" ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63742)) | Waiting won't bring cheaper loans soon |
| Prices | ₹9,354 per sq ft in H1 2026, up 9% in a year ([Knight Frank](https://content.knightfrank.com/research/3116/documents/en/india-real-estate-office-and-residential-market-h1-2026-12927.pdf)) | Rising, not falling |
| Unsold stock | 72,800 units in Q1 2026, up 12% in the quarter and 24% in a year ([ANAROCK](https://assets.credai.app/public/knowledge_center_document/1778232199205_Bengaluru_Q1_2026_Residential_Market_Viewpoints_84d41ec09a.pdf)) | More choice and room to negotiate |
| Sales | 16,450 units in Q1 2026, down 5% in the quarter, up 10% in a year (ANAROCK) | Demand steady |
| New project approvals | 309 in January to September 2026, against 387 a year earlier, across seven taluks ([our K-RERA count](https://acrenkey.com/insights/new-rera-registered-projects-bengaluru-by-taluk-2020-2026/)) | Fewer new launches ahead |
| Affordability | EMI-to-income 35% in H1 2026 ([our affordability study](https://acrenkey.com/insights/home-loan-affordability-index-for-bengaluru-2016-2026/)) | Stretched but under 50% |

The signals point both ways. Rising stock helps buyers negotiate. Rising prices and a rate rise make waiting more expensive. At Q1 2026's sales pace, 72,800 units is about 13 months of stock.

## Is waiting for a rate cut worth it?

Probably not in the near term, given the RBI's October stance. And even when cuts come, a small price rise cancels them.

## Worked examples

These use a ₹90 lakh flat, an 80% loan over 20 years, and the approved 8.5% illustrative rate.

### Worked example 1: what a year's wait costs at different price growth

| Price growth in the year | Price after a year | Extra price | Extra stamp charges at 7.6% | Total extra |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0% | ₹90,00,000 | Nil | Nil | Nil |
| 5% | ₹94,50,000 | ₹4,50,000 | ₹34,200 | ₹4,84,200 |
| 9% (Knight Frank's last year) | ₹98,10,000 | ₹8,10,000 | ₹61,560 | ₹8,71,560 |

Waiting only pays if prices fall, or if you'd otherwise buy the wrong home in a hurry. You'd be paying rent either way.

### Worked example 2: a rate cut vs a price rise

| | Buy now at 8.5% | Wait for 8.0% |
|---|---|---|
| Loan | ₹72,00,000 | ₹72,00,000 |
| EMI | ₹62,483 | ₹60,224 |
| Monthly saving | | ₹2,260 |

Now let the price rise while you wait. At 8.0%, ₹62,483 a month services a loan of about ₹74,70,000, which is 3.75% more. So if prices rise more than 3.75% before the cut arrives, waiting leaves you worse off. Prices rose 9% last year.

## When does waiting make sense?

- You haven't saved the down payment plus about 7.6% for charges.
- Your job or city may change within three years.
- You haven't chosen an area, or haven't seen it in the monsoon (see our [flood-prone areas guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-flood-prone-areas-map-from-ksndmc-data/)).
- The project you like has no RERA registration or approvals yet. Our [K-RERA step-by-step](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/how-to-check-any-bengaluru-project-on-rera-karnataka-step-by-step/) shows how to check.
- You'd have to stretch past half your income for the EMI.

## The READY test (our framework)

| Letter | Question | Ready if |
|---|---|---|
| R: Reserves | Do you have the down payment, charges and six months of expenses? | Yes, without borrowing from family at short notice |
| E: EMI headroom | Can you pay the EMI at your rate plus 2%? | Yes, under about half of take-home pay |
| A: Area | Have you settled on two or three areas and checked water and flood? | Yes |
| D: Duration | Will you stay at least five to seven years? | Yes, so costs are spread out |
| Y: Your job | Is your income stable for the next few years? | Yes |

Five yeses: market timing matters less than finding the right home. Two or more noes: waiting is sensible, whatever prices do.

## Will a guidance value revision change the timing?

Possibly, for a few buyers. A revision of 12% to 15% was reported as being prepared in February 2026 ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/karnataka/2026/Feb/21/guidance-value-yet-to-be-hiked-by-15-across-karnataka-expect-property-rates-to-go-up), news). Our position: the 2023 rates are still in force; check Kaveri. Stamp duty is charged on the higher of price and guidance value, so a revision only matters where the new guidance value would exceed your price. Our [stamp duty calculator](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/stamp-duty-and-registration-calculator-karnataka/) shows the difference.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is 2026 a good time to buy property in Bengaluru?

It depends on your readiness. Rates rose in October 2026 and prices rose 9% in a year, but unsold stock is growing, which helps you negotiate.

### Will property prices fall in Bengaluru?

There's no sign in the data. Knight Frank's average rose 9% in a year to H1 2026. Rising unsold stock could slow growth in some areas.

### Should I wait for home loan rates to fall?

The RBI raised repo in October 2026 and signalled no near-term cuts. Even when cuts come, a price rise of 3.75% can cancel a 0.5-point cut.

### How much does waiting a year cost?

On a ₹90 lakh flat, about ₹8.7 lakh including stamp charges if prices rise 9%, and about ₹4.8 lakh at 5%. Nothing if prices stay flat.

### Does rising unsold inventory mean prices will drop?

Not necessarily. It means more choice and room to negotiate. ANAROCK counted 72,800 unsold units in Q1 2026, about 13 months of sales.

### When should I definitely wait?

If you lack the down payment and charges, may move cities soon, haven't chosen an area, or would need more than half your income for the EMI.

### Does a guidance value revision affect when I should buy?

Only if the new value would exceed your price, since duty is charged on the higher of the two. Check Kaveri for the current value.

### How long should I plan to stay to make buying worthwhile?

At least five to seven years is a common rule of thumb, so stamp charges and other costs are spread out. Shorter stays favour renting.
