# New RERA-registered supply in Bengaluru by taluk, 2020–2026: where are projects being added?

**Short answer:** New RERA projects in Bengaluru are concentrated in three taluks. From 2020 to 15 Oct 2026, Karnataka RERA approved 2,692 residential, plotted and mixed projects across seven Bengaluru taluks. Bengaluru East (643), Anekal (603) and Bengaluru South (556) made up two-thirds. January–September 2026 approvals were 20% below the same months of 2025.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **2,692 approvals** in seven taluks from 2020 to 15 Oct 2026, commercial projects excluded.
> - **East, Anekal and South lead,** with about 24%, 22% and 21% of the total.
> - **2026 is slower:** 309 approvals in Jan–Sep against 387 a year earlier. Hosakote was the exception, up from 30 to 40.
> - **The outskirts are mostly plots:** 78% of Devanahalli's approvals and 52% of Hosakote's are plotted developments, against 8% in Bengaluru East.
> - **Projects, not homes.** The register's list view doesn't give unit counts, so this counts projects.

Supply numbers from consultants count launched units across the city. This study uses the regulator's own register instead, taluk by taluk, so you can see where new registered projects are actually being added near you. The table behind it is published with this page.

## How did we count?

- **Source:** the Karnataka RERA "View All Projects" list ([K-RERA](https://rera.karnataka.gov.in/viewAllProjects?language=en)) for seven taluks: Anekal, Bengaluru East, North and South, Yelahanka (Bengaluru Urban), and Devanahalli and Hosakote (Bengaluru Rural). Captured 15 Oct 2026.
- **Counted:** projects with status APPROVED, by the register's "approved on" date, 1 Jan 2020 to 15 Oct 2026. Residential/group housing, plotted and mixed developments; commercial excluded. Rejected, withdrawn, revoked and transferred entries are left out.
- **Not counted:** units, sizes or prices. The list view doesn't show them, and we don't name projects or promoters.
- **Duplicates:** removed by acknowledgement number. The same base list gives 4,529 approved non-commercial projects in all years, matching our [RERA completion-date study](https://acrenkey.com/insights/do-east-bengaluru-projects-deliver-on-their-rera-date/).

Approvals dipped in 2020–21, picked up from 2022, and peaked in 2025 at 505. Hosakote has already passed its 2025 total in 2026 (45 against 40). Devanahalli and Yelahanka, both on the airport side of the city, more than doubled between 2020 and 2025.

## Did registrations slow in 2026?

Yes, in most taluks. Comparing the same nine months:

| Taluk | Jan–Sep 2025 | Jan–Sep 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anekal | 99 | 58 | −41% |
| Bengaluru South | 76 | 60 | −21% |
| Bengaluru East | 75 | 68 | −9% |
| Bengaluru North | 42 | 36 | −14% |
| Devanahalli | 39 | 29 | −26% |
| Yelahanka | 26 | 18 | −31% |
| Hosakote | 30 | 40 | +33% |
| **All seven** | **387** | **309** | **−20%** |

It comes as unsold stock in the city rose 35% in a year by ANAROCK's count, though consultants still counted Q3 launches above sales (see our [Q3 2026 market report](https://acrenkey.com/insights/bengaluru-residential-market-report-q3-2026/)). Registration counts can also move with how fast the authority processes applications, which this data can't separate.

At the 2026 pace, the seven taluks would finish the year at roughly 35 × 12 = 420 approvals, below every year since 2022 except 2024 (421). That's a projection, not a count.

## The Count–Kind–Clock check (our framework)

Before you treat "supply" as good or bad for a purchase:

1. **Count.** How many registered projects in your taluk, and is the number rising or falling?
2. **Kind.** How many are the same type as yours: flats, plots or villas?
3. **Clock.** When are they due? Check each competing project's current completion date on RERA, and its record of extensions.

| Taluk | Approved projects with a current completion date in 2027 | In 2028 |
|---|---|---|
| Bengaluru East | 106 | 81 |
| Bengaluru South | 88 | 61 |
| Anekal | 79 | 63 |
| Bengaluru North | 37 | 41 |
| Devanahalli | 36 | 23 |
| Yelahanka | 22 | 16 |
| Hosakote | 17 | 22 |

Completion counts cover all approved non-commercial projects, whatever year they were approved, using the register's current proposed date.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many new RERA projects were registered in Bengaluru in 2026?

Across seven Bengaluru taluks, Karnataka RERA approved 333 residential, plotted and mixed projects between 1 Jan and 15 Oct 2026. In January–September there were 309, against 387 in the same months of 2025, a 20% fall.

### Which part of Bengaluru has the most new RERA projects?

Bengaluru East taluk, with 643 approvals from 2020 to 15 Oct 2026. Anekal (603) and Bengaluru South (556) follow. Together the three make up about two-thirds of the 2,692 approvals across the seven taluks we counted.

### Why does Devanahalli show so many projects?

Because most are plots. 188 of Devanahalli's 242 approvals since 2020, about 78%, are plotted developments. Only 53 are flat or group-housing projects. Compare like with like before reading Devanahalli's numbers as apartment supply.

### Does this data show the number of flats being built?

No. The K-RERA list view shows projects, their type, status and dates, but not unit counts. A small plotted project and a large apartment complex each count as one. Consultants' launch figures count units but cover new stock differently.

### Is new supply slowing in Bengaluru?

On registrations, yes, in 2026. Six of seven taluks approved fewer projects in January–September 2026 than a year earlier. Hosakote was the exception, up 33%. Approval counts can also reflect processing speed at the authority, which this data can't separate.

### How can I check new projects near me?

Use the Karnataka RERA "View All Projects" search and filter by district and taluk. Each entry shows the project type, status, approval date, original and current completion dates and any extensions. Our RERA step-by-step guide shows how to read it.

### Why are commercial projects left out?

This study is about homes. Commercial projects don't add housing supply, so we exclude them. Mixed developments are kept because they include residential units.

### Can I download the data?

Yes. The CSV published with this page gives approvals by taluk, year and project type from 2020 to 15 Oct 2026. It holds counts only, with no project or promoter names, and it's built from the K-RERA list pages captured on 15 Oct 2026.
