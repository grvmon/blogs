# Living in Banashankari: rates, metro, water and flood risk

**Short answer:** Banashankari property rates depend heavily on the stage. Guidance values run from ₹1,55,500 per sq m on KR Road in 2nd Stage to ₹81,000 in 3rd Stage and ₹38,000 to ₹56,000 in most 6th Stage blocks. Knight Frank and ANAROCK don't track Banashankari separately. The Green Line stops at Banashankari.

> **Key takeaways**
> - **No consultant figure:** neither Knight Frank nor ANAROCK publishes a Banashankari price, so use guidance values and recent registrations.
> - **Guidance values by stage:** 2nd Stage cross roads ₹1,04,000 per sq m, 1st Stage ₹71,000, 3rd Stage ₹81,000, 5th Stage ₹62,000.
> - **Metro running:** the Green Line has stations at Banashankari, JP Nagar and Yelachenahalli.
> - **Two corporations:** 2nd Stage's PIN 560070 is South; 560050 and 560085 are West.
> - **Our STAGE check:** stage row, title from BDA, approach and drains, Green Line walk, extra floors.

Banashankari is a large BDA-planned area in south-west Bengaluru, laid out in six stages from Kanakapura Road out towards the ring roads. The inner stages are older and built up; the 6th Stage is newer and more spread out. This guide uses official records and named sources, and says where data runs out.

![Map of Banashankari showing the six stages, Kanakapura Road, the Outer Ring Road and the Green Line stations](/img/blog/banashankari-map.png)

## Who governs Banashankari?

Two corporations. Banashankari 2nd Stage's PIN 560070 is 100% South. PINs 560050 and 560085, covering Kathriguppe, Srinivasa Nagara and nearby wards, are 100% West. All are inside GBA, so stamp duty, cess, surcharge and registration add up to 7.6%. Check your plot with our [ward and corporation lookup](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/which-ward-and-city-corporation-is-my-flat-in-gba-pin-code-lookup/).

## What are property rates in Banashankari?

### Market prices

Knight Frank's H1 2026 report and ANAROCK's 2026 reports don't list Banashankari. The nearest Knight Frank benchmark is Kanakapura Road, at ₹5,300 to ₹10,100 per sq ft, up 6% in a year ([Knight Frank](https://content.knightfrank.com/research/3116/documents/en/india-real-estate-office-and-residential-market-h1-2026-12927.pdf)). Treat that as context only: the inner stages are older and more central. Portal averages are listing prices, not registrations.

## Is there a metro in Banashankari?

Yes. The Green Line runs along Kanakapura Road on Banashankari's eastern side. Its extension from Yelachenahalli to Silk Institute opened on 14 January 2021 ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=1688594)).

| Line | Nearby stations (station names per news reports) | Status, 19 Oct 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Green Line | Banashankari, JP Nagar, Yelachenahalli, Konanakunte Cross | Running |

The western stages, especially 6th Stage, are a long way from the line. Our [metro lines guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-metro-lines-2026-status-of-every-line-for-homebuyers/) covers planned lines too.

## Where does Banashankari get its water?

The inner stages are older BDA layouts with long-standing BWSSB connections. The outer stages and nearby villages are more mixed, and some depend on borewells and tankers. The Bengaluru South and RR Nagar zones are both covered by Cauvery Stage 5, which by May 2026 had connected about 1.15 lakh of an estimated 1.8 lakh buildings in the 2007 villages ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/karnataka/bengalurus-water-woes-piped-connections-sanchari-cauvery-reduce-tanker-demand/article70978412.ece), news). Ask for the BWSSB connection and recent bills; our [Cauvery Stage 5 guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/cauvery-stage-5-coverage-which-areas-get-bwssb-water/) explains how.

## Is Banashankari flood-prone?

We didn't find a 2026 official list naming Banashankari locations. In April 2026, the South City Corporation had 24 of the city's 101 listed flood-prone locations and the West had 48 ([The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/bengaluru/2026/Apr/11/52-of-101-flood-prone-locations-in-bengaluru-yet-to-be-fixed-before-monsoon), news). Those figures cover each whole corporation, not this area.

Low streets, cellars and plots near old tank beds are the ones to check. See our [lake and drain checks](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/flood-risk-lake-buffers-and-rajakaluve-checks-before-you-buy/) and [flood-prone areas map](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-flood-prone-areas-map-from-ksndmc-data/).

## Worked examples

These use published figures and illustrative prices.

### Worked example 1: the same price in two stages

A 30 × 40 ft (1,200 sq ft) site agreed at ₹1,20,00,000.

| Step | 2nd Stage cross road (₹1,04,000) | 6th Stage, 6th Block (₹40,000) |
|---|---|---|
| Guidance value | ₹1,15,94,203 | ₹44,59,309 |
| Price ÷ guidance value | 1.03 | 2.69 |
| Charges at 7.6% (on price) | ₹9,12,000 | ₹9,12,000 |

In 2nd Stage, the price is only 3% above the floor, so a slightly higher row would put duty on the guidance value. In 6th Stage, a price 2.69 times the floor is worth testing against recent registrations nearby.

## The STAGE check for Banashankari (our framework)

| Check | Question | Where to check | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stage row | Which stage, block and site row applies? | Kaveri, sale deed | Seller quotes an inner-stage rate for 6th Stage |
| Title from BDA | Was the BDA lease-cum-sale converted to an absolute sale deed? | BDA allotment, possession certificate, sale deed | Lease-cum-sale period not closed |
| Approach and drains | Does the street hold water, and is the plot near an old tank bed? | Visit after rain, drain maps | Water marks on the compound wall |
| Green Line walk | How long to the nearest station? | Walk it at peak hour | More than 20 minutes with no feeder |
| Extra floors | Were floors added beyond the sanctioned plan? | Plan sanction, OC | No OC for upper floors |

Score each check green, amber or red. Two reds and you should walk away or renegotiate. Our [legal checks guide](https://acrenkey.com/blogs/bengaluru-property-legal-checks-from-title-to-oc/) covers BDA title and OC.

## Banashankari buyer's checklist

- [ ] Confirm the ward, corporation and khata.
- [ ] Find the exact stage, block and site row on Kaveri.
- [ ] Work out the price-to-guidance ratio.
- [ ] Check the BDA allotment and absolute sale deed.
- [ ] Check the plan sanction and OC.
- [ ] Visit after heavy rain.
- [ ] Time the trip to the nearest Green Line station.
- [ ] Ask for BWSSB bills and tanker spend.

## What mistakes do buyers make in Banashankari?

- **Treating it as one market.** Guidance values vary four-fold between stages.
- **Using portal averages as prices.** They're listings, not registrations.
- **Skipping the BDA paper trail.** Allotment, possession and absolute sale deed all matter.
- **Assuming the metro is close.** The western stages are far from the line.
